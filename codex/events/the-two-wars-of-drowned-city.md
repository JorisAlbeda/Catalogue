# The Two Wars of Drowned City

## Description

At Drowned City, Rowan Voss and Iona Ashe named their wars to each
other's faces rather than let the other guess at them. Rowan declared
she means to take the Woodland Empire whole, site by site, until no
throne remains for any Chancellor to answer from — a war waged from
beyond the Empire's own borders. Iona answered that conquest wasn't an
accounting, and declared instead a rebellion raised from within the
Empire's own halls, aimed not at erasing the throne but at making
whoever sits it answer for what it's buried. Neither backed down from
the other, and neither pretended the two paths wouldn't cross before
either was finished.

## History

First spoken aloud at Drowned City, early in that same war, witnessed by both
retinues' own warbands making camp along the city's walls.

## Location

Drowned City
