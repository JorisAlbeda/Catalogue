# The Audience at Magician's Code

## Description

A confrontation between Marren Wick, claimant to the vacant Truthwatcher's desk at Charming Valley, and Chancellor Lilith Woodborne, held in the main chamber of Magician's Code. Marren asked Lilith to formally close the century-old, never-finished ledger entry on Sorrel Wick's death. Lilith refused and declared the Truthwatcher post closed outright rather than address it, ending the audience.

## History

In the 410th year of the Old Oak, while Lilith reinforced Charming Valley's defences, Marren requested and was granted an audience. Her consort Ivan named Sorrel Wick a traitor before Lilith could stop him — a reaction sharper than the century-old case seemed to warrant. Marren left without the signature she asked for, but with both Lilith and Ivan visibly unsettled, and a growing conviction that the Woodborne family was guarding more than the Darkest Flame.

## Location

Magician's Code, Charming Valley
