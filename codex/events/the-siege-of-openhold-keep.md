# The Siege of Openhold Keep

## Description

The largest battle the Openlands have seen since the Firestorm at Buried Giant, and the first time the Darkest Flame has been lit in anger since. Wynn Ashe's Cairn Watch, joined by Perrine Oswick's own Tally Company under Aldous Ferry, spent weeks breaking against Openhold Keep's gate before the Flame ended the fight in minutes — and ended nearly both companies doing it.

## History

Teagan Sedge had held the Openhold Plains since Round 3, the gate taken by bribe and the Wallwatch broken in the fight that followed. Wynn Ashe came for it in Round 4, carrying two companies' worth of warbands into the assault — his own Cairn Watch out of Buried Giant, and Aldous Ferry's Tally Company out of Shrouded Wood, neither of them so much as leaving their own ground to join the fight. The gate held through everything thrown at it in the ordinary way. It didn't hold against the Darkest Flame.

Wynn broke the defense outright once the Flame was lit, killing Ren Ashby and the Farrow Company where they stood. Both of his own companies were spent doing it — Aldous Ferry died leading the Tally Company's own share of the breach, and what came home to Buried Giant afterward was a single warband, all that survived of either company. Teagan Sedge was taken alive, and the tome she'd spent the season chasing — the Darkest Secret, the same page that first taught the Flame its own use — went with Wynn when he withdrew.

He didn't hold the ground two days. With nothing left to garrison it, bandits walked through the same gate his own men had just broken, and Teagan slipped free of him in the confusion. The Openhold Plains answers to neither Openhold nor Teagan now.
