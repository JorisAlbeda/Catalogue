# Great Slum

## Description

Great Slum sprawls across the Hinterland's low ground, houses stacked on houses with no single roofline agreeing with its neighbor — the kind of place too big and too poor for any one authority to have gotten around to claiming. Traders, refugees, and everyone with a reason to be seen by nobody in particular pass through it on the road between the Woodland Empire and Ashvale.

## History

Held loosely by its own bandits rather than any named power, until Rowan Voss, Warden of Ashvale, brought her company through it in the second month of the war, the same day Iona Ashe took the Great Spire. In a tavern reeking of ale she found Bram Hollis holding court over a circle of naysayers, and took him on rather than argue with him — then turned her whole company on the bandits running the rest of the place. The fight came down even; she paid for the win with one warband of her own, and the two who survived it now hold the slum outright.
