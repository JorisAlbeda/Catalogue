# Ashvale Mine

## Description

The Ashvale Mine was a place of quiet labor and unspoken tensions, where the weight of past decisions lingered in the air. Once part of the Woodland Empire, it later was a source of great wealth to Ashvale after the Truce.

## History

Ashvale Mine was once a contested territory, claimed by those who sought to control its resources and the power it represented. In the war against the Woodland Empire, Perrin and Corvin fought each other over control of the Mine's riches — the last and hardest of their rivalry's battles, and the one that finally convinced both of them the other wasn't worth losing more men to. Perrin secured the site, but the truce struck here outlasted the fight itself: rivals turned neutral, then allies, then more than that. The camp he set up afterward grew into an active mining town once peace had settled over Ashvale.

Some three generations back a family of oath-smiths, the Marrows, took the high ground above the workings and raised a forge there — the ones who swear a binding word into a blade as they set its temper. The arrangement that grew up between the two was the closest thing Ashvale had to an armoury: what came down the quarry road as the Mine's ore went back up it as sworn iron, the kind that still bites once it crosses onto Empire ground. Ashvale called it the Mine on its own maps; everyone who worked it called it the quarry.

Both ends of that arrangement stopped inside a single season. The Growing Wood reached the cut around Year 539 and folded the whole of it in — no ore, no mining town, no quarry road left to walk. The forge on the mountain above has been idle ever since, and the mountain answers to the name Oathforge now.

## Location

Ashvale's own ground once — the workings now lie under the Growing Wood, below Oathforge in the Hinterland.
