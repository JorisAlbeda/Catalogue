# Ashvale Mine

## Description

The Ashvale Mine was a place of quiet labor and unspoken tensions, where the weight of past decisions lingered in the air. Once part of the Woodland Empire, it later was a source of great wealth to Ashvale after the Truce.

## History

Ashvale Mine was once a contested territory, claimed by those who sought to control its resources and the power it represented. In the war against the Woodland Empire, Perrin and Corvin fought each other over control of the Mine's riches — the last and hardest of their rivalry's battles, and the one that finally convinced both of them the other wasn't worth losing more men to. Perrin secured the site, but the truce struck here outlasted the fight itself: rivals turned neutral, then allies, then more than that. The camp he set up afterward grew into an active mining town once peace had settled over Ashvale.
