# Greyrock Pass

## Description

Known as Narrow Pass for most of its recorded history — a defile in Ashvale, the only passage between Ashvale and the Woodland Empire (now the Second Woodland Empire). Renamed sometime in the decades after the Openhold war; Ashvale's own maps and travelers call it Greyrock Pass now, after the pale stone the road cuts through, though older residents and older records still know it by the old name. The pass became a strategic stronghold, where bandits and warbands established their presence, making it a contested ground. A Giant Python, later tamed by Lilianne, made its lair within the pass, guarding its secrets. The pass was long ruled by bandits under Daz the Bandit Witch.

## History

Before Ashvale was founded, Narrow Pass was a critical route through the Hinterland. Lilianne travelled here on a quest to find the Bandit Crown. It was here that she was dubbed The Bandit Queen by Daz, gaining her the allegiance of all bandit clans.
Perrin eventually chased the bandits out of the pass, and after the truce, it became the official border between Ashvale and the Woodland Empire.
It is speculated that the Narrow Pass is the most important reason that Ashvale was never invaded during the war with Lilianne, or in the century of relative peace that followed.

Renamed Greyrock Pass sometime after Year 492, following the rise of the Second Woodland Empire on its western border. Worth remembering by both names — older accounts, relics, and travelers from before the rename still only ever call it Narrow Pass, and that's not an error when they do.
