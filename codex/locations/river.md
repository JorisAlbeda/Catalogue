# Fairwater

_Formerly the River._

## Description

A wide slow ford where the Provinces road has always had to cross, shallow enough to walk and broad enough that nobody crosses it unseen. Low stone buildings stand along both banks in various states of abandonment, most of them older than whoever last held the crossing. The water runs clear and cold and is the only thing in the region that never seems to change hands.

## History

Whoever controls the ford controls the road, which is why the ford has been controlled by somebody for as long as there has been a road, and why none of them have lasted. The bandits who held it most recently took it the ordinary way and lost it the same way — they simply stopped being worth crossing for, and then one week they were gone.

Marek Sten took it in the fifth month of 540 with seven companies and nothing at all in reserve behind them, having stripped half the garrison off his own held ground to do it. The fight lasted a week and was not close; the river bandits and the crew keeping watch on the Ancient City three regions off both went to hiding on the same news. One company of his own went down and he has not said whose. He named it Fairwater the day it fell, and told anyone who asked that it was the first fair hand the map had dealt him and he would not insult it by pretending otherwise.

## Location

The Provinces

## Garrison

Three companies of the Fair Hand under Korr Vane, who has now been left holding two different pieces of ground in two months and is beginning to suspect this is a permanent condition rather than a run of luck.
