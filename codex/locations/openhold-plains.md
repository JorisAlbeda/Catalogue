# The Openhold Plains

## Description

Open country in Openhold, unnamed on any older map — empty ground nobody at court had ever bothered to claim or call anything, until Lilith Woodborne stumbled onto it and named it herself, for lack of anything better. Marren Wick gave it a real name once the keep and the town around it were hers to name: Openhold, after the ledger she swore to keep open here, over the throne, over everything else. Its own technology lags behind its neighbours by design more than by lack — nothing sees issue here until it's been tested, logged, and sworn to, same as everything else in Openhold.

## History

Claimed originally by Lilith Woodborne while campaigning through the Provinces, and taken from her by Marren Wick, who named it Openhold after the ledger she swore to keep open here. The Openlands never grew past this one ground in the generations that followed — every Truthwatcher after Marren left the wider Provinces exactly as unclaimed as she did, and what grew instead was Openhold itself, packed tighter with every generation. The gate changed hands more than once in living memory, held briefly by the Exile Teagan Sedge and then broken in the largest siege the Openlands have seen since the war (see "The Siege of Openhold Keep"), with bandits holding the ground for a time after. The wider Openlands confederation didn't survive its own Truthwatcher's death; Openhold itself did, folded into the Second Woodland Empire as a vassal state rather than swallowed outright — a walled town that still keeps its own Truthwatcher's seat, well past the point of being fought over.

## Present day

Decades on, the ground has never gone back to any single banner. What trade still crosses it answers to Openhold in name, the same as Openhold itself now answers to Heart of the Oak — a quiet ground, well past the point of being fought over. What garrison keeps the gate these days still trusts a sworn blade over anything untested — Openhold's own ledgers keep as careful an account of who's sworn to what weapon as they ever did of coin, and little that hasn't been logged and blessed that way sees issue. The current Truthwatcher answers to the Emissary in name and mostly in practice, though not entirely in private — the Growing Wood presses on Openhold's own border the same as anyone's, and the seat has quietly begun to wonder whether Heart of the Oak wouldn't be safer in different hands. Senna Coldwell took that seat this year, the desk having sat unclaimed since Perrine Oswick — the first to hold it in forty-eight years.

## Garrison

Ren Ashby's Farrow Company died to the last defending the gate against Wynn Ashe's siege; whoever held the ground after was whatever bandit band walked in behind him once he withdrew. The Wallwatch itself — the Openhold Plains' own standing gate-guard — went twenty-some years without a captain after Dunstan Reeve died at Great Slums, the post handed down to no one in all that time. Senna Coldwell picked it back up a piece at a time and holds it openly now, the first since Dunstan; Perce Cutter runs the gate day to day in her stead since she moved to the Truthwatcher's own desk this year. The four who followed her out of the ranks to serve her personally — the Spearbound, led by Tavin Aldren — are recorded separately as her own company.
