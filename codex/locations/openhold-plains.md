# The Openhold Plains

## Description

Open country in the Provinces, unnamed on any older map — empty ground nobody at court had ever bothered to claim or call anything, until Lilith Woodborne stumbled onto it and named it herself, for lack of anything better. Marren Wick gave it a real name once the keep and the town around it were hers to name: Openhold, after the ledger she swore to keep open here, over the throne, over everything else.

## History

Discovered by Lilith Woodborne while she was campaigning through the Provinces. She built it up as a personal holding, trading with the itinerant Wild Cry and the traders of Tinker's Fair, and kept two of her prizes here — the Circlet of Command and the Darkest Secret — behind only a handful of guards.

Marren Wick broke those guards here, took the ring from Lilith's own head, and forced open the chest Ivan had been keeping close since Magician's Code. What was inside — the tome that tells how to conjure the Darkest Flame — has been hers since. She's held the ground rather than withdraw with it, growing her own following of traders and recruits on the same open country the Chancellor once claimed for her own.

An old, half-fallen keep stands on the ground as well, long squatted in by a bandit clan. Marren drove them out and set a stonemason to rebuilding it — the first ground she's ever ruled outright, not inherited or claimed by title.

Rowan Voss brought Ashvale's own column against that keep in the fourth month of the war, reaching for the ring itself. The walls held. She withdrew a warband short of what she'd marched with, and left Tamsin Reed behind for good.

In the years after the war's end, the ground took the name Openhold — Marren Wick's own choosing, and the first name this place ever had that wasn't just a placeholder for whoever happened to be standing on it. The keep took the name with it, Openhold Keep from that day on. The town growing up around it, and the wider country answering to it, are known together now as the Openlands: no throne, no Empire, just the ledger Marren swore to keep open, and whoever's willing to be judged by the same one she is.

Eighty-some years on, the Openlands have never grown past this one ground — every Truthwatcher since Marren, including the cautious Bevan Stroud, left the two stretches past Salt Flats exactly as unclaimed as she did. What's grown instead is Openhold itself, inward: the same walls Marren raised, packed tighter every generation since, until Tinker's Fair's stalls stand close enough to touch and no family's business stays its own for long. A place that's prospered for two generations without ever needing to expand also hasn't had anywhere new to walk in that whole time.

That closeness broke for the first time in living memory when Teagan Sedge, the Exile holding Salt Flats, walked into the Openhold Plains uninvited and left with a piece of Tinker's Fair's own trade to show for it. She didn't stop there — Wild Cry and Tinker's Fair were both gone within the hour, scattered by a trick out of the old world's own keeping and replaced by whatever came back blind from it. Openhold Keep itself never moved; locked doors don't answer to that kind of trick. For the first time since Marren raised these walls, Openhold's own ground stands nearly empty of the hands that built its trade — one keep still standing, sealed, while the rest scatters and regrows around it.

She came back for the rest of it before the season was out. Whoever holds Openhold Keep's own gate took her coin easily enough, but the Wallwatch didn't fold the same way — Dunstan Reeve's own people held their ground harder than anything the Openhold Plains has seen since Marren first cleared it, and it cost the Exile more of her own company than the flats ever did to finally break them. The Openhold Plains flies her banner now, keep and gate both, the first time in eighty years this ground has answered to anyone but Openhold.

The banner didn't fly long. Wynn Ashe's own Cairn Watch broke the gate the following month in the biggest siege this ground has ever seen, ending it with the Darkest Flame itself once the walls finally gave — Ren Ashby and the Farrow Company holding the gate didn't survive it, and Teagan Sedge was taken alive. Wynn held the ground only long enough to strip it of what he'd come for; with nothing left of his own company to leave standing guard, he sent the last of it home to Buried Giant. Bandits walked in within two days of the gate standing empty, and Teagan slipped free in the same confusion. The Openhold Plains answers to neither Openhold nor any Exile now — just whoever's squatting in the keep this season.

## Location

Cradle

## Garrison

No standing garrison. Ren Ashby's Farrow Company died to the last defending the gate against Wynn Ashe's siege; whoever's holding the ground now is whatever bandit band walked in behind him once he withdrew. The Wallwatch itself — the Openhold Plains' own standing gate-guard, led by Dunstan Reeve, whose own father and grandfather held the same post before him — answers to Openhold still, wherever Dunstan Reeve has pulled what's left of it back to.
