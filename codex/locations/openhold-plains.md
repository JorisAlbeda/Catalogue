# The Openhold Plains

## Description

Open country in Openhold, unnamed on any older map — empty ground nobody at court had ever bothered to claim or call anything, until Lilith Woodborne stumbled onto it and named it herself, for lack of anything better. Marren Wick gave it a real name once the keep and the town around it were hers to name: Openhold, after the ledger she swore to keep open here, over the throne, over everything else. Its own technology lags behind its neighbours by design more than by lack — nothing sees issue here until it's been tested, logged, and sworn to, same as everything else in Openhold.

## History

Claimed originally by Lilith Woodborne while campaigning through the Provinces, and taken from her by Marren Wick, who named it Openhold after the ledger she swore to keep open here. The Openlands never grew past this one ground in the generations that followed — every Truthwatcher after Marren left the wider Provinces exactly as unclaimed as she did, and what grew instead was Openhold itself, packed tighter with every generation. The gate changed hands more than once in living memory, held briefly by the Exile Teagan Sedge and then broken in the largest siege the Openlands have seen since the war (see "The Siege of Openhold Keep"), with bandits holding the ground for a time after. The wider Openlands confederation didn't survive its own Truthwatcher's death; Openhold itself did, folded into the Second Woodland Empire as a vassal state rather than swallowed outright — a walled town that still keeps its own Truthwatcher's seat, well past the point of being fought over.

## Present day

Decades on, the ground has never gone back to any single banner. What trade still crosses it answers to Openhold in name, the same as Openhold itself now answers to Heart of the Oak — a quiet ground, well past the point of being fought over. What garrison keeps the gate these days still trusts a sworn blade over anything untested — Openhold's own ledgers keep as careful an account of who's sworn to what weapon as they ever did of coin, and little that hasn't been logged and blessed that way sees issue. The current Truthwatcher answers to the Emissary in name and mostly in practice, though not entirely in private — the Growing Wood presses on Openhold's own border the same as anyone's, and the seat has quietly begun to wonder whether Heart of the Oak wouldn't be safer in different hands.

That same Truthwatcher has since set a plain toll into the town's own ledger: nobody deals on Openhold's ground for free unless they already answer to the gate — the town's own people go on doing business as they always have, while everyone else pays for the privilege.

Cradle

## Garrison

No standing garrison of its own for years, since Ren Ashby's Farrow Company died to the last defending the gate against Wynn Ashe's siege — whatever bandit band walked in behind him once he withdrew held the ground for a time after. That's changed this year: Senna Coldwell, having finally claimed the Wallwatch's own command, has begun posting the Spearbound — the company she raised under Tavin Aldren — at the gate itself rather than keeping it entirely on her own board.
