# Threadbare

_Formerly the Wastes._

## Description

A stretch of the Provinces long left off every map worth the name — ground nobody bothered to claim because nothing grew there worth claiming. Flat, dry, and until this year unremarkable, the kind of place a traveller crosses rather than stops in. It is neither empty nor quiet now.

## History

Held by whatever bandits happened to be passing through, same as most of the unclaimed Provinces, for as long as anyone's kept count. Nothing is recorded of it before Year 540 — it simply wasn't worth writing down.

That changed when Fia of the Ampa brought Memory of Nature to the ground itself and the land answered faster than anyone watching expected: fae creatures flocking to soil that held nothing living the season before. A whistle-shaped relic turned up in the same stretch of ground, its own maker and purpose both still unknown.

Marek Sten came at it twice. The first time he brought two warbands and the ground turned him back inside a week, and he went away short a company and shorter on patience. The second time he did not bring soldiers to begin with — he gave a knee-high stranger a seat at his fire without looking up, and got a thumb's width of green thread stitched to his sleeve for it. Inside the week four companies wearing that same green came up out of the long grass and the standing water: centaur-kin off the high country, mire-things nobody has a settled name for, and something that came out of the river which the survivors decline to describe. What Fia had woken on that ground found itself facing older things than itself and went to hiding rather than to grave. Sten named the flat Threadbare the same day and has told anyone who asked that this is a plain description of what his claim to it is made of.

## Location

The Provinces

## Garrison

Four companies of the green thread, led by Bramwen of the Long Grass — who came because the road was there, not because anyone sent her, and who asks roughly once a week whether he still wants them standing where they're standing.
