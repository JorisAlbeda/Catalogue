# Oathforge

## Description

A hard climb above the Hinterland's own quarry road, high enough that an attacker declaring against anyone here fights at a real disadvantage — the ground itself favors whoever's already standing on it. Forge-smoke still hangs over the upper switchbacks some mornings, thin now compared to what it used to be. Bandit-held, and has been since long before the quarry below it fell quiet.

## History

Home to a line of oath-smiths for three generations, forging the binding-word blades that hold their temper even on Empire ground — the working theory behind why an oath-sworn weapon bites true where a plain one doesn't (see `timeline.md`, "Iron That Won't Bite"). The quarry the forge served was folded into the Growing Wood in a single season, leaving the forge with no ore to work and no soldiers of their own left to swear in.

Cade Marrow, the forge family's own third generation, left Mountain over exactly that loss and swore the Vow of Beastkin, meaning to force the Empire back far enough that the forge has something to strike again.

Cade returned this season and drove the squatters off the forge yard by force, losing one of his own line in the taking. Digging under the forge floor itself turned up a jar of Sticky Fire, buried long enough that even the family who worked the ground above it never knew.

Cade renamed it Oathforge the same week he retook it — no longer just the mountain his family happened to work, but the thing his own oath was actually sworn over.

## Garrison

The Ironbound Line, Cade Marrow's own company, led by Sera Hollow.
