# Ancient City

## Description

A dead city at the far edge of the Hinterland, old enough that nobody living claims it — no banner, no garrison, nothing left of whoever built it but the shape of streets under the overgrowth and a relic still waiting under the rubble for a crew willing to dig it out.

## History

Discovered by Iona Ashe, in the fourth month of the war, having pushed her column past every mapped edge of the Provinces looking for whatever the wider world still had buried in it. The relic there was never recovered.

## Location

Hinterland
