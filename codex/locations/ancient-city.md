# Ancient City

## Description

Ruin-ground in the Hinterland, old enough that nobody living remembers whose city it was before it stopped being one — walls still standing in places, streets still traceable under the growth, but nothing left worth calling a roof. Two clear building plots remain within the old wall line, enough for a fresh hand to raise something new on ground that already knows how to hold weight. Bandits keep loose watch over it rather than living in it outright.

## History

The city's fall predates anything in living memory or the Marrow family's own records — only the walls and the streets survived whatever ended it. Iven Marrow challenged Chancellor Aldric Vane's own rule alongside Selene Claire in Year 150 of the Old Oak, and family telling has it he passed through these same ruins during that campaign, finding them worth a second look he never got to give — the war went to Selene Claire instead, and Iven's own line went quiet in the histories not long after. Whatever he meant to come back for stayed buried under the ruin for close to four centuries.

Cade Marrow found it this season, flipping the ground while passing back through the Hinterland from the Provinces — the first of his own line to actually set foot inside the walls Iven only rode past. A jar-sealed harp turned up among the old relic-count still sitting there, seemingly untouched since the city fell.

It changed hands in the fifth month of 540 without a single company marching into the Hinterland to do it. Marek Sten called the bandits at the Provinces ford and the bandits keeping watch on these ruins in the same breath, on the reasoning that they were one lot and one fight, and the ford went so badly for them that the ruin's own crew were gone before anyone reached the walls. They heard, and that was enough. Sten has still never set foot inside the city and has said plainly that he will not name ground he has not walked, so it keeps the name it has had for four centuries — the only site he holds that he did not rename within the day.

## Location

The Hinterland

## Garrison

Three companies of riverside hearth-folk under Nell Brack, sent east with no instruction past *hold it*. There has been a fire burning in the old wall line since the day they arrived, which by the reckoning of the people who lit it is the first the streets have had since the city stopped being one.
