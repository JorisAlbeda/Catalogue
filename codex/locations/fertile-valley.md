# Fertile Valley

## Description

Fertile Valley was the capital of Ashvale, known for the Boiling Lake and the Ruined Temple, a remnant of a time when it was the most important location in the Supreme Empire.

## History

Fertile Valley was first contested by Corvin and Perrin Ashe, who both claimed the land without prior agreement. Corvin's attempts to secure the valley led to conflicts, including battles against bandits and rival claimants. Perrin later used the valley as a rallying point, gathering companies under his banner. The Ruined Temple was later used as a meeting point for several important discussions about the future of the land.

Neither Corvin's nor Perrin's line ever fully cleared it of the bandits that came after — the ground still answers to them rather than to Ashvale. Cade Marrow reached it this season, pushing north out of Ancient City with a company raised the same week and throwing them at the valley's own garrison within days of being raised at all. The garrison held. He pulled his people back rather than spend the rest of the company taking ground he couldn't have kept regardless. He came back for it a month later with the valley's own farm folk guiding him, and took it — the first time Ashvale's capital has answered to anyone but bandits since the Wood reached the Mine.

## Garrison

The Gatebound, Cade Marrow's own company, led by Tam Ferris — ruin-dwellers out of Ancient City now standing alongside the valley's own farm folk who marched with them.

## Location

Ashvale
