# Widowmire

_Formerly Marshes._

## Description

A low, waterlogged stretch of the Provinces past Great Slum's own rooftops, where the ground gives more than it holds. Mushrooms taller than a person grow wild here, their faces uncannily expressive, sunk into a running conversation with each other that nobody outside the swamp has ever fully followed. Caravans still cut through it on the old road, drivers who've learned to talk fast and keep moving — faster still, now, since word got round of what actually chased the bandits' own warbands out.

## History

Discovered by Rowan Voss, Warden of Ashvale, in the third month of Rowan Voss's invasion, the same season she turned away the Chancellor's second offer of shared thrones. Passing over the mushrooms as too strange a partner to be of much use, she took on a caravan-broker instead. She dug a cup free of the mud there — old enough that even the mushrooms seemed to give it space — trading one of her own long-kept secrets for it.

Correction: the gang running the Marshes' back streets was never actually driven out — Rowan mistook a broker's welcome for the whole ground's loyalty. By the fourth month of Rowan Voss's invasion she'd recognized the gap and pulled her people out of the Marshes entirely rather than settle it, redirecting them south toward Plains instead.

Taken at last by Marek Sten, who dug a rusted cauldron free of the same mud Rowan once wouldn't touch and marched on the bandit hold squatting the ground the same day. The bandits broke fast enough, but whatever else lives in that mud didn't — his soldiers came back from the fighting swearing something screamed like a kettle and moved like no bandit does, and two of the three warbands he sent in didn't come back at all. He renamed the ground Widowmire before the mud had even dried on his boots, and hasn't corrected anyone who's called it haunted since.

## Location

The Provinces

## Garrison

The Waybound, down to the one warband that walked back out of the mud, still led by Ivy Cade — holding the ground more out of stubbornness now than the fair fight she signed on for.
