# Marshes

## Description

A low, waterlogged stretch of the Hinterland past Great Slum's own rooftops, where the ground gives more than it holds. Mushrooms taller than a person grow wild here, their faces uncannily expressive, sunk into a running conversation with each other that nobody outside the swamp has ever fully followed. Caravans still cut through it on the old road, drivers who've learned to talk fast and keep moving.

## History

Discovered by Rowan Voss, Warden of Ashvale, in the third month of the war, the same season she turned away the Chancellor's second offer of shared thrones. Passing over the mushrooms as too strange a partner to be of much use, she took on a caravan-broker instead. She dug a cup free of the mud there — old enough that even the mushrooms seemed to give it space — trading one of her own long-kept secrets for it.

Correction: the gang running the Marshes' back streets was never actually driven out — Rowan mistook a broker's welcome for the whole ground's loyalty. By the fourth month of the war she'd recognized the gap and pulled her people out of the Marshes entirely rather than settle it, redirecting them south toward Plains instead.
