# Shrouded Wood

## Description

A stand of old-growth forest in the Hinterland, canopy thick enough that noon light reaches the ground the color of dusk. The trees grow close and deliberate, less like a wild wood than a garden someone stopped tending generations ago and let keep growing anyway. Paths through it don't stay where a map last put them.

## History

Openhold's own foresters stopped logging this stretch three generations back, after two separate crews reported the same thing independently: trails that led back out exactly where they'd gone in, no matter which way they walked. Nobody official ever explained it, and nobody official has gone back in since. The bandit clans who drifted through the Hinterland left it alone too, by the same reputation.

Tinney Woodborne walked in as a self-declared emissary of the Old Oak and, by all local account, walked back out again the same day knowing exactly where every path led — the first outsider anyone can name who has. Whatever the wood recognized in that, it hasn't tried to turn Tinney around since.

Perrine Oswick marched on it directly, the first time she's sent warbands past the Openhold Plains' own walls since taking the desk — Ossian Fenwick's own forest-sense the only reason the company found the clearing at all through the wood's own permanent fog. The fighting cost her most of the company she brought, but the ground and the vote both came down in her favor by the day's end, whatever the local mood about it afterward.

The walled garden her own soldiers had coaxed back to life in the fighting's aftermath didn't survive the season. Word reached the camp that the Openhold Plains itself had fallen the same day, and whatever discipline was holding the company together came apart with it — fights by afternoon, two deserters caught by evening, and by night a larger crew had torn a hole clean through the garden's own walls. The beasts that had been kept out got in and finished what the deserters started; by morning there was nothing left of it but a wrecked stand of plants. It took a full day of more ordinary soldiering — watches, discipline, the usual — to put the camp back in order. Forest Council's own ground held regardless; locked things don't answer to that kind of unrest any better here than they do at Openhold's own Keep.

Tinney Woodborne came back through the fog two mornings after, alone and unescorted, and named what the fighting had actually cost the wood — wolves and boars dead alongside the men, a nursing mother among them. Perrine didn't deny it. What came out of that meeting is the closest thing the wood has to a standing accord under its new garrison: nothing built or cleared at Shrouded Wood without the Forest Council's own word first, its surviving wildlife left alone, and coin promised toward new growth where the fighting tore the ground up. Whether Aldous Ferry's company actually holds to it is Tinney's own business to watch now, by their own word.

## Location

Hinterland

## Garrison

None left standing. The Tally Company marched to Openhold Plains whole and didn't come back at all — spent to the last warband breaking Openhold Keep's own gate alongside the Cairn Watch, Aldous Ferry with them. The wood's own local sentiment still ran toward the emissary they'd voted out even while Aldous held it; whether that sentiment outlasts an empty garrison is an open question now.
