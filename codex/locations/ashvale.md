# Ashvale

## Description

A country neighbouring the Woodland Empire where the Hinterland used to be. It is a self-governed entity with its own borders, roads, harvests, and a ruling line that keeps the story of the mask. The region is marked by the Ruined Temple, where its history was shaped by the truce between the Empire and its former territories. Its own arms and tools skew old and mended rather than new — a country too stretched economically to chase whatever advances its neighbours can afford to gamble on.

## History

Formed after Lilianne's war against Sorrel Wick's rebellion, Ashvale was established through a truce where the Empire relinquished control of Fertile Valley, Mine, Narrow Pass, and Wastes to a new name. Perrin Ashe, with Corvin Varn at his side — a rival turned ally turned, by the time Ashvale stood, something closer to a partner — unified these lands under Ashvale's banner. The Ruined Temple, the last remains of the Empire's former rule, became a symbolic meeting ground for Perrin and Corvin. Ashvale's histories focus on the legacy of Corvin Varn, the Fire Talkers' debates over the buried flame, and the unclaimed Mask in the Reliquary, which questions the meaning of symbols versus their deeds. What little coin the country can spare for its own soldiers goes toward keeping old, oath-sworn arms in service rather than commissioning new ones — a frugality that's left Ashvale's border garrisons, closest of anyone to Greyrock Pass, with the surest hands-on sense of what does and doesn't hold once it crosses into Empire ground. Thrift is the kinder half of that account. The Mine fed an oath-forge on the mountain above it that had supplied the country's sworn arms for three generations, and when the Growing Wood took the cut around Year 539 it took the forge's ore with it — so there has been nothing new to commission since, whatever Ashvale could or couldn't afford. The blades its garrisons carry were sworn to somebody's grandfather.
