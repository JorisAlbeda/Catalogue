# Heart of the Oak

## Description

What was once called Great Slum: houses stacked on houses along the Hinterland's low ground, grown up now around the healed garden and the Old Oak at its center rather than around any single ruler's hall. The seat of the Second Woodland Empire, and the busiest pilgrim road in the Provinces, both at once — the old sprawl never went away, it just grew a shrine at its middle and never stopped growing outward from there.

## History

Held loosely by its own bandits rather than any named power, generations back, until Rowan Voss brought her company through it during her own invasion of the Woodland Empire, the same day Iona Ashe took the Great Spire in a separate rebellion. She took Bram Hollis on as an adviser there and broke the bandits running the rest of the place, though what she held never grew past a rough camp in her own lifetime.

Generations later, in the Openhold war, the ground past its own edge held a garden Tinney Woodborne came to protect — dying at first, then healed, then the site of the war's own final battle. Tinney died defending it and the Old Oak took what was left of them in rather than let the death be the end of the story. Pilgrims started walking the road within the season, kneeling at the roots the way Openhold once knelt to a ledger.

## Present day

Decades of pilgrims have done what no single ruler managed in Great Slum's own long history: given the sprawl a center. The garden's grown into a proper seat of governance, root and bark as much as building, and Tinney Woodborne rules from it still — uncontested, in fact as well as name. The old name persists in old mouths and old records; most who live there now call it only Heart of the Oak.
