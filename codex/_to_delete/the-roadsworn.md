# The Roadsworn

## Description

Two warbands of horse out of the dry reach east of Ashvale, riding for Cade Marrow on a wage and a swearing rather than any loyalty to the Hinterland. Led by Halla of the Dry Reach, a khan who has spent most of her adult life contesting the same thin grazing with Bramwen of the Long Grass and knows exactly how Bramwen's people ride, camp, and break. They came to Fertile Valley with everyone else who wanted to shake the hand of the smith who took Ashvale's capital back; Halla stayed after the others had gone, because Cade was the first man in years to offer her something worth more than grass.

## History

Raised at Fertile Valley in Year 540, out of the crowd that came up the valley road to thank Cade Marrow for the liberation of Ashvale's capital. Halla named her price in a single sentence and Cade paid it without argument. They rode east the same week, toward Ancient City and the marsh-lord holding it — the first company Cade has carried on the road since the Gatebound were left standing in the valley's fields.

## Location

Riding with Cade Marrow, Hinterland

## Voice

Halla says the useful half of a thing and leaves the rest. Talks about people the way she talks about horses — what they can carry, how far, and what they do when they are tired — and means no insult by it. Names a price once and does not haggle, on the reasoning that a man who needs the second offer was never going to pay the first.
