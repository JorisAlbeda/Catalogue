# The Gainsaid

## Description

Two warbands of Ashvale malcontents who have spent years telling anyone who would listen that the Growing Wood is a conquest rather than a season, and have been moved on from three towns for saying it where tax collectors could hear. Led by Orla Tind, who arrived at Fertile Valley with the crowd that came to thank Cade Marrow and stayed behind to tell him the thanking was premature. None of them are soldiers. All of them are used to being unwelcome, which turns out to travel well.

## History

Raised at Fertile Valley in Year 540, out of the delegations that came up the valley road after Ashvale's capital was taken back from the bandits. Orla Tind's opening argument to Cade was that a freed capital is worth nothing if whoever holds it starts behaving like whoever held it before; he agreed with her and hired her on the spot, which she has not entirely stopped being suspicious about.

## Location

Traveling with Cade Marrow, Hinterland

## Voice

Orla argues in questions she already knows the answer to, and waits out the pause rather than filling it. Reaches for the plain word where a softer one is on offer — takings, not tribute; conquest, not growth. Has never once called Cade "lord" and shows no sign of starting.
