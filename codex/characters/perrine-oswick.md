# Perrine Oswick

## Description

Perrine Oswick keeps a slim, closed ledger with her always, and a measuring cord at her belt that was her mother's before it was hers, worn smooth at the knot. Ink stains sit permanently grey-black in the creases of her hands. She dresses plainly, in a dark buttoned coat cut for use rather than court, and carries herself like a woman used to being watched by neighbours who've known her family for three generations — which she is. By a reputation earned over a decade on the road, she is incapable of letting a lie stand unchallenged, even a small, kind one.

## History

Perrine was born inside Openhold's own walls, which haven't moved since Marren Wick raised them eighty-some years ago — the Hold has only grown inward since, denser each generation, until Tinker's Fair's stalls stand close enough to touch and everyone's ledger is common knowledge whether they like it or not. Her father, Joss Oswick, kept the town's granary ledgers, exacting to a fault, the kind of honest that won't round a measure even when a crowd wants him to. Her mother, Ede, ran the mending stall at Tinker's Fair and taught Perrine to read a scale the same honest way, warmer than Joss's but no less unbending. Perrine spent a decade after that riding circuit as a tally-rider, settling boundary disputes across Cradle with nothing but her mother's cord and a reputation that never bent.

She took up the Truthwatcher's seat meaning to finish something that outlived Marren Wick herself: the two stretches of ground past Salt Flats that every Truthwatcher since has left unclaimed, including Bevan Stroud, who held this desk for the thirty-one years before Perrine and coined the phrase Openhold still repeats — that the ledger records the world and doesn't go looking for more of it. Perrine means to prove him wrong without repeating Marren's own method: not by marching on that ground, the way Marren took this desk in the first place, but by opening an honest, standing account for the bandit clans already holding it — a real stake in Openhold's trade, tallied and paid out exactly as promised — trusting her own audited reputation to succeed where a throne's word never has. She's turned over the old story of the Bandit Crown more than once, the relic that once let bandits ally with the Empire outright under Lilianne with no war at all, unrecovered since and unplaced on any map — and wonders whether it's still sitting somewhere in the same unclaimed ground she means to go see for herself.

## Location

Plains, Cradle

## Status

Active

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain and exact — reaches for a specific number or place-name before an adjective every time. Verbal habit: repeats back whatever she's just been told, word for word, before she'll answer it — a tally-rider's old habit of confirming a claim before she's willing to act on it. Emotional default: steady and watchful; rarely raises her voice, even when naming a lie outright. Speech rhythm: measured, unhurried, willing to let a silence sit rather than fill it with something she hasn't checked yet.
