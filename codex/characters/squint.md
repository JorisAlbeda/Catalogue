# Squint

## Description

Small for her age, and she'd tell you so herself before anyone else got the chance — a mess of a dozen kids' own hand-me-downs, a bent copper whistle worn on a cord, and eyes that never seem to land on the person she's actually talking to, always somewhere past their shoulder. Answers to nobody's whistle but her own.

## History

Led the loose pack of gate-runners who'd already turned Openhold's back alleys into their own private relay system long before anyone official asked them to — passing word door to door faster than any rider could, mostly for the fun of it, sometimes for a coin. When the Wallwatch's call to arms went out this year, most of Openhold answered with spears; Squint answered with the same whistle-code her own crew had been using for years, just pointed at the gate instead of at each other. Senna took the offer seriously rather than sending the children home, which is more or less the only reason Squint took the offer seriously back.

## Location

Openhold Plains

## Voice

Vocabulary level: quick, clipped, streetwise — says a thing once and expects you to have caught it. Verbal habit: answers a question with what she saw rather than what she thinks it meant, leaving the thinking to whoever asked. Speech rhythm: fast starts, sudden stops, like she's already listening for the next whistle before she's finished the last sentence.
