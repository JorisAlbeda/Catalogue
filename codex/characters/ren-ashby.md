# Ren Ashby

## Description

A rangy, sun-worn soldier who's spent more years standing gate-watch than he'd like counted, with the Wallwatch's own plain grey still showing under a stitched-on scrap of Ashvale's colors he clearly didn't ask permission to sew. Answers questions straight and doesn't volunteer more than he's asked — the habit of a man who's spent a long career reporting to whoever happens to be behind the desk that season.

## History

Ren left Ashvale's own lower ward as a young man, chasing steadier pay than border garrison work ever offered, and found it at the Openhold Plains under three Truthwatchers running — Openhold's own coin never once missed a season, whatever else changed hands. He kept family back home the whole time: a sister who never left, a nephew he's only met twice. When the gate finally gave and the banner going up over it turned out to be Ashvale's own colors worn by an Exile rather than some Provinces war-band he'd never have recognized, something about that landed harder than the defeat itself did. Openhold paid on time. It never once asked after Ashvale. He put his name in with the others willing to talk, and didn't have to think as long about it as he expected to.

He held the same gate for Teagan Sedge that he'd once held for Openhold, and didn't walk away from it a second time — the Farrow Company died holding it when Wynn Ashe turned the Darkest Flame on the breach.

## Location

Plains, Cradle

## Status

Deceased — killed defending Openhold Keep's gate
