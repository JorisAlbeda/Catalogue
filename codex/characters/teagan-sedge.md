# Teagan Sedge

## Description

Calloused hands and a long scar along one forearm from a raider's blade that didn't quite miss. Wears scavenged Ashvale watch-company leathers over her own company's plain grey sash, a fire-scorched signal-horn — pulled from her watchtower's ashes this spring — slung at her hip in place of the one that burned with it. Watches a room the way she watches a tree line: for whoever hasn't moved yet.

## History

Born to a family three generations displaced from Salt Flats, raised in Ashvale's watchtower camps and conscripted young into the labor gangs rebuilding the border forts, rising to captain the company that held them. Grew up doubting her great-grandmother Rowan Voss's old lesson — that reaching for a throne wasn't worth the cost — until a bandit raid burned her own watchtower and cost her Aleyn Farrow, her second-in-command, who held the gate alone so the rest could fall back. She stopped patching the symptom after that.

That spring of Year 492 she marched on the bandit banner squatting Salt Flats and took it back in one committed push, renaming what was left of her company the Farrow Company. Within the month she took the Openhold Plains outright — bribing past Openhold Keep's gate, then breaking the Wallwatch — the first ground ever taken from Openhold rather than bandits. She lost it again before the season turned: Wynn Ashe's siege broke that same gate, Ren Ashby's company died defending it, and Teagan was taken alive when the Darkest Flame ended the fight.

She slipped free two days later when bandits overran the emptied ground, and walked back to Salt Flats to rebuild from the one ground she still held. Days after Openhold's own fall, she found Wynn at a tavern table and made him give the whole account — Tinney's death and rebirth, Perrine's killing, her own plea for Perrine's life gone unanswered. Asked if he'd light the Flame again knowing the cost; he said yes. She named him for it — a weapon, not a man — and left him with a look of pity rather than a last word.

She never took Openhold's throne, and once Tinney settled into it, never knelt to that one either — she made Salt Flats a country of its own instead, Farrowhold, named for Aleyn. Ruled it the rest of her working life, handed the Farrow Company to hands she trusted once her knees stopped trusting her, and never spoke Wynn's name at Salt Flats again.

## Location

Farrowhold, Provinces

## Status

Active — old now, ruling Farrowhold in name more than in daily
business, having long since handed the Farrow Company to hands she
trusts. Still to be found at Salt Flats: the country's own elder, and
a first-hand account of the Openhold war for anyone who asks rather than a
story handed down secondhand.

## Voice

Vocabulary level: simple — field-command language, no flourish, no rank she hasn't earned herself. Verbal habits: gives even ordinary requests as flat declaratives, never as questions ("Get water," not "could you get water"); when genuinely rattled, goes quiet and counts something under her breath instead of speaking — paces, torches, whatever's in front of her. Emotional default: guarded and coiled, calm because she's decided to be, not because she is. Speech rhythm: clipped, short sentences — the one exception is Aleyn Farrow's name, which she starts and doesn't finish.
