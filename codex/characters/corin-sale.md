# Corin Sale

## Description

A fast-talking broker out of the old Tinker's Fair circuit, more comfortable sealing a promise than a purse. Sets up shop wherever there's room and a crowd wanting something, and has a habit of getting people to swear to things they'd rather not, worded so carefully they don't notice until later.

## History

Placed by Iona Ashe at Plains, Round 3 — planted in Lilith Woodborne's own dooryard on the strength of Bryn Holt's knack for finding room to grow things.

## Location

Plains
