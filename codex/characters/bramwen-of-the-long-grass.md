# Bramwen of the Long Grass

## Description

Grey through the flank and slower than she was, with a winter-scarred foreleg she does not favour where anyone can see her do it. A band of faded green stitching is worked into the hair at her shoulder, old enough that the colour has gone almost to grey with the rest of her.

## Voice

Answers the question actually asked and stops. Uses the plain word for things — *dead*, *cold*, *far* — and lets other people supply the softer one if they want it.

## History

Her grandmother took Tib's thread the summer the high grass burned, and wore it out of that country and into the next one, and the herd has carried it since without much discussing why. Bramwen has worn hers since she was a filly. Among her kind it is not thought of as a bargain and there is nothing anyone considers outstanding about it; it is closer to a road you were born beside, which you may or may not walk, and which is there regardless.

She has walked it three times in her life. The third was out to a bandit-ridden flat in the Provinces where a Farrowhold exile she had never met was standing, wearing a thumb's width of the same green on his sleeve. She brought the herd out herself rather than send them, looked at the man once, and asked him where he wanted them — which he did not have an answer ready for.

## Location

Threadbare, Provinces
