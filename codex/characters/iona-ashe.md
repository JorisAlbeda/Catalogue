# Iona Ashe

## Description

Iona Ashe is sun-browned and rangy, dark hair cropped short for
travel. She wears Corvin Varn's old coat, too wide through the
shoulders and patched twice at the elbow — the scrap of grey-green
cloth he sewed into the lining is still there. She counts things off
on her fingers when she's working out what's owed, and treats an
unanswered question the way Corvin was said to: as unfinished work —
except she's taken it further than he ever did. A quiet grievance
doesn't read as settled to her, only as unattended.

## History

Iona Ashe descends from Perrin Ashe, first Warden of Ashvale and
Corvin Varn's closest companion in the years the country was built.
When the Varn signet ring left the family in a leaner year, Corvin's
coat passed to the Ashes instead, and its stitched-in habit — never
call a debt settled just because it's gone quiet — became hers along
with it. She set out from Ashvale as its newest Exile, arriving at
Buried Giant, where she recruited the relic-hunter Doran Krell as her
adviser, Round 0.
