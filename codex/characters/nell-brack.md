# Nell Brack

## Description

Sun-cured and square-handed, with a cook's forearms and a permanent squint from thirty years of standing over heat. Carries a flat iron griddle strapped across her back where a soldier would carry a shield, and has been asked about it exactly once by anybody who then went on asking.

## Voice

Talks while doing something else, and stops talking when she stops working. Measures distance in how long a thing takes to cook and time in what meal it is. Says "sit down" to people who are upset, and means it as an instruction.

## History

Fed the riverside for most of her life out of a low stone house at the ford, where the road crossed and everybody crossing had to stop anyway. The bandits who took the crossing did not burn her out or turn her over; they simply stopped paying, then stopped asking, and after enough years of that there was no house left worth the word — she walked out of it herself one autumn and did not look back, and has never once described where she went instead.

She came back to the water this year without meaning to, and stopped because there was woodsmoke on the air and cooking in it, from a fire nobody had lit. That was Memory of Home, played to the bank by a Farrowhold exile an hour earlier, and she was not the only one it brought in. She walked into his camp with two companies' worth of riverside folk behind her, all of them following the same smell, and asked him whose kitchen it was.

He sent her east with three companies and no instructions past *hold it*, into a ruin four hundred years cold that she had never seen and he still has not. She has a fire going in the old wall line within a day of arriving, which the people who came with her say is the first one those streets have had in living memory or well past it.
