# Bryn Holt

## Description

A wiry forager who came out of the Spire's ruin with nothing broken and everything noticed, a rabbit-skin pouch of seeds and small stolen things slung at her hip. She has a knack for finding what's still willing to grow in ground everyone else has given up on, and doesn't ask what a cause is called before deciding whether to help it take root.

## History

Recruited by Iona Ashe among the stragglers left behind after the Chancellor turned the crowd against her at the Great Spire, Round 3.

## Location

Buried Giant

## Status

Active
