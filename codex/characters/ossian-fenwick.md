# Ossian Fenwick

**Description:** A stooped, owlish old road-warden, wrapped in a faded blue travel-cloak he's had re-stitched more times than he'll admit. Carries a single walking staff worn smooth at the grip, and always seems to be listening for something past the treeline before he answers. Doesn't hurry, on the road or in conversation.

**History:** Ossian spent nearly forty years walking the roads between Cradle's outlying farms as an unofficial toll-warden, long before anyone at the desk was young enough to notice him — guiding traders around bandit patrols for whatever they could spare, and keeping no ledger of his own beyond what his memory held. He buried a wife at Salt Flats the year before it fell outright to the bandit clans, and hasn't gone back since, though he'll never say so outright if asked. When word went round that the newly-seated Truthwatcher wanted someone who actually knew the back roads rather than just the maps of them, half of Plains gave her his name before she'd finished asking.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Voice:** Plainspoken, no scholarly flourishes. Verbal habit: answers most questions with a specific memory of a specific stretch of road ("There's a bend past the third ford where the Bandits never bother looking") rather than a direct opinion, and measures distance in walking-time, never miles. Emotional default: unhurried and watchful. Speech rhythm: measured, with a pause before anything that matters.
