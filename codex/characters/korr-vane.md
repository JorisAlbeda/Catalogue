# Korr Vane

## Description

Broad through the shoulders even for mountain-bred stock, with a bare patch scarred pale across one forearm where something in Widowmire's own mud caught him and didn't let go easy. Carries himself like a man who's already been the last one standing once and doesn't intend to make a habit of it — checks his own footing twice before anyone else's.

## History

Came down off the high country with the rest of the yeti-kin crew Marek Sten raised at Widowmire, and was one of two sent out to test the Wastes for him. Watched the other go down where he stood and walked the rest of the way back alone with nothing to show for it — the kind of return that could have ended his standing with the company instead of starting it, if Marek hadn't decided a man who comes back to report a loss plainly is worth more than one who doesn't come back at all. Put in charge of the company once it had been built back up properly, and meant to bring it home whole the next time out. He did — the second push at that ground cost him nobody at all, which he has not entirely forgiven himself for enjoying, given who it cost instead.
