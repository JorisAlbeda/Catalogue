# Ivy Cade

## Description

A caravan driver going grey early, forearms corded from a lifetime of reins and rough road. A barter-scar blisters pale across one palm where a Marsh mushroom's spores caught her once, years before she learned to wrap her hands first. Cuts a fresh notch into the underside of her own wagon seat for every safe crossing — more habit than record, ever since the ground started shifting the far end of her route without warning.

## History

Born on the Marshes road itself, 511 of the Old Oak, to a caravan family that never kept a fixed address long enough for a birth to happen anywhere else. Ran the last regular route through the Marshes for a decade, hiring Marek Sten on and off despite his habit of vanishing mid-route without a word, and gambling that he was worth the risk again each time. Watched the Growing Wood start taking a season's worth of the road's far end every year, and watched Farrowhold's own protests about it go answered with silence. Lately carries more in the wagon than her own manifest admits to — most of it his — and has decided not to ask what exactly she's not asking about. When Marek finally asked her crew to throw in with him properly, not just for one run's pay, she named her price high and took the deal anyway.

Lost two of every three she brought to the ground he renamed Widowmire the same day she got it, to something that wasn't the bandits and didn't die like them either. Stayed anyway, with the one wagon crew she had left, holding the mire itself instead of just driving past it.

The Empire came for it in the fourth month of that year, and there was never a version of that week where one crew held a marsh against the Chancellor's own line. She had crossed that ground safely for ten years and owned it for four months, and she did not treat those as the same thing; the people who were there say she put the wagon across the road herself rather than leave the crossing open, which was habit as much as tactics and cost her the same either way. Her crew went down on the far side of it. The Empire's colours went up before the week was out.

The wagon seat is still where she left it, notched underneath a decade deep. Nobody has moved it, and the Empire's own garrison has taken to walking around it rather than past it.

## Status
