# Marek Sten

## Description

A scar splits two knuckles on his right hand from a card table that ended badly. A satchel worn soft at its false bottom rides at his hip, and it never once shows what it's actually carrying. Doesn't blink first — not across a table, not in a doorway search.

## History

Born in Farrowhold the year the salt-crust first showed green along the riverbank, 517 of the Old Oak, and never settled long with any one caravan crew or trade after that — moved goods and messages through the Marshes' back channels from the time he was old enough to carry a pack that looked lighter than it was, learning early which guards could be paid and which couldn't.

Picked up cards in the caravan camps along the same routes, and it stuck — reads a bluff faster than most people read a face, and has never once walked away from a hand he thought he could still win. Carried a hidden message to Teagan Sedge at Salt Flats once, as a boy, tucked into his pack's false bottom — she found it before he'd said a word, said nothing to the guard at her own gate, and told him afterward, plainly, that a country was only a promise nobody had broken yet. He has turned that over more than he'd say.

Watched Farrowhold's own council send the Emissary protest after protest about the wood swallowing their northern fields, answered each time with silence and more trees. Decided somewhere on one of those routes that petitions were a council's tool, not his — that holding something the Empire couldn't afford to lose would get an answer faster than asking ever would, and that he'd deal with whoever could get him there, a bandit banner or an Empire agent alike, so long as they held their end.
