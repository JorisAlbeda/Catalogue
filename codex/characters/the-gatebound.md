# The Gatebound

## Description

Two warbands raised inside an hour, at Ancient City's own broken gate, out of people who'd never carried a spear for anyone before Cade Marrow asked. Ruin-dwellers and scavengers mostly — the ones who'd made their own quiet homes in those old streets long before the fire, the chase, or the fighting over either ever reached them. None of them owe the Empire, Openhold, or the bandits who used to squat there any particular loyalty; they owe the man who called for hands instead of taking them. Led by Tam Ferris, a scavenger who knew every collapsed cellar in the ruin worth hiding supplies in before he ever led anyone through one.

## History

Raised the same day Cade Marrow walked free of a week in chains at Ancient City — the watching he did in that ruin, bound and asked after every morning, was apparently argument enough for people who'd learned to judge a stranger by what he did under pressure rather than what he said before it. Marched north with him the same hour, into the Hinterland's own last unclaimed ground.

Half the company didn't come home from Fertile Valley, Ashvale's own bandit-held capital, reached the same week they were raised — Tam pulled the survivors back through a hedge row rather than spend what was left of them holding ground the retreat had already made impossible to keep.

Two warbands' worth of Fertile Valley's own came in behind them a month later — farm folk off ground the bandits had been sitting on without ever working, who know every hedge row in the valley the Gatebound first walked into blind.

Took the valley on the second attempt and lost a whole company to it — eighty-odd dead in one morning on the north side, while the rest came round behind. What survives of the Gatebound has been left standing in those fields rather than marching on with Cade; they are Fertile Valley's garrison now, and half of them were farming it a month ago.

## Location

Garrisoning Fertile Valley, Hinterland

## Voice

Tam Ferris gives directions the way he'd have once talked someone through a bad stretch of rubble — short, practical, more concerned with where the ground gives way than with anything that sounds like an order. Doesn't raise his voice around Cade; watched him too closely for a week to need convincing twice.
