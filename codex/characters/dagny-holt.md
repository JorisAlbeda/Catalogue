# Dagny Holt

## Description

A wiry, sun-cracked rider who's spent more nights in the saddle than out of it, one ear always cocked for hoofbeats that aren't her own. Keeps a horn slung across her back that she's never once needed to blow twice.

## History

Rode patrol for a grain caravan out of Ashvale before word reached her that Buried Giant was hiring hands who meant to keep the ground rather than pass through it. Arrived within the week and took to riding the perimeter the same way she'd ridden escort — just for a company that finally stayed put.

## Location

Buried Giant, Hinterland

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain, economical, mostly about ground and distance ("half a day out," "riding double"). Verbal habit: reports what she's seen before anyone asks, unprompted, as if the telling itself is the job. Emotional default: alert, unbothered by threat itself, only by surprise. Speech rhythm: clipped, arrives at the point immediately.
