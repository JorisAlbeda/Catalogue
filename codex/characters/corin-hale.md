# Corin Hale

## Description

An old drillmaster gone grey on the road, more often found sitting with the wolf that trails him than with the soldiers he's come to see. Carries no weapon worth the name — just a battered oath-book, and the wolf, which sits when he sits and rises when he rises, and never once has to be called.

## History

Corin spent decades drilling garrisons that answered to the Hold in name only — scattered, half-forgotten postings that never once mustered together, let alone fought together. He took to walking between them himself, unpaid and mostly unasked, reminding each one that the company at the next post over answers to the same banner and owes them the same debt in a fight. Most garrisons humor him. A few have actually held to it. He turned up at Buried Giant's own gate not long after the bandit banner came down, looked over the ground a while, and told Wynn Ashe plainly that a company this size, holding ground this important, ought to know it isn't alone — wherever else the Hold's own warbands are standing, they're his to call on too, if he only asks it right.

## Location

Buried Giant, Hinterland

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain, soldier's shorthand — companies, postings, musters, never anything grander. Verbal habit: addresses the wolf mid-sentence, half to it and half to whoever he's actually talking to, as if it understands the point better than the person does. Emotional default: patient past the point most people would call it stubbornness — he's been ignored by better men than whoever's listening now. Speech rhythm: slow, repetitive, willing to say the same thing three ways until it lands.
