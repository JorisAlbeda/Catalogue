# Dessa Vann

## Description

Leads the Tin Troubadours, the stage name her small travelling company of players goes by — the Acting Troupe is the trade the card itself is named for, the Tin Troubadours is what they call themselves. Her act is worth more than the coin in their cap — she can dress a plain deal up as something grander, or a grand one down as something forgettable, whichever the room needs. Trades in favor and rumor as readily as in performance.

## History

Newly counted among Fia's retinue as of Round 1, when her troupe's true trade — an Arcane act that plays as Beast or Order at the bargaining table — was named openly for the first time.

Already noticed by the same Round: Fia takes the Tin Troubadours' read of a room to her own strategy meetings before anyone else's, and their advice has been sound every single time so far — sound enough that it's stopped looking like luck. Nobody's asked Dessa outright who actually writes the Troubadours' scripts. Dessa hasn't offered.

## Location

Shrouded Wood, Cradle

## Voice

Vocabulary level: mixed, switching between a performer's flourish and a trader's plain terms. Verbal habit: answers a direct question with a line that sounds rehearsed before landing the real point underneath it. Emotional default: amused, unbothered. Speech rhythm: flowing.
