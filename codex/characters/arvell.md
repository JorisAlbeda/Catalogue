# Arvell

## Description

Leads the Rangers, a company of goblins recruited to Fia's service out of the World Deck's own drift of free companies — a small, wiry-fast band that Arvell himself doesn't remotely resemble. He's a troll, grizzled and bearded, who speaks mostly in grunts, growls, and the occasional single word when a grunt won't quite cover it. Quick to anger and impossible to miss coming — heavy-footed, loud-mouthed, the opposite of what anyone would pick for scouting work. And yet, ordered by Fia specifically, the whole company turns shockingly quiet and hard to see, Arvell included, as if the noise were a choice all along. The company doesn't work for coin; Fia pays them in jokes, told to Arvell directly, and he's never once asked for anything else.

## History

Took up with Fia's retinue in Round 1, recruited faceup at Shrouded Wood the same month the Brass Horse was recovered there.

## Location

Shrouded Wood, Cradle

## Voice

Vocabulary level: minimal — grunts and growls first, single words when he bothers. Verbal habit: answers with a grunt or a jerk of the head before any word comes, if one comes at all. Emotional default: short-fused, quick to bristle. Speech rhythm: blunt, clipped.
