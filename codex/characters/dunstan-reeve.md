# Dunstan Reeve

## Description

Dunstan Reeve has held the gate at Plains under three Truthwatchers now, and looks it — a thick-necked, grey-stubbled man who still does his own rounds at night rather than trust a junior watchman's ears. Keeps his own small company, the Wallwatch, drilled to the same plain standard it's kept since before Bevan Stroud's own time: nothing showy, nothing that hasn't already been tested. He answers to whoever sits the desk, not to any one Truthwatcher personally, and says so plainly if anyone asks.

## History

Dunstan's father held the same post before him, and his father's master before that — the Wallwatch has never once been raised fresh in living memory, only handed down, drilled the same way, generation on generation, the same unhurried thoroughness Openhold itself has settled into. He's buried two Truthwatchers he served under and expects to see out a third; the office changes hands more often than the gate does. Perrine Oswick is, so far, the first in his memory to actually ask him a direct question about the Wallwatch's own numbers rather than simply trusting the gate was held.
