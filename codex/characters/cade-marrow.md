# Cade Marrow

## Description

A smith's build gone a little soft since he left the forge, old burns still marking the left knuckles. Carries his father's oath-hammer, its head worn smooth where a word used to be stamped in — he can still feel the shape of the letters under his thumb, though the iron's long since stopped showing it to anyone else. Doesn't raise his voice; a smith who shouts over a quench never hears the iron crack in time.

## History

Third generation of a family of oath-smiths working the high forges above the Ashvale Mine — the ones who swear a binding word into a blade as they set the temper, the reason an oath-sworn weapon still bites true on Empire ground and a plain one doesn't. Took up the hammer at fourteen for his own reason, not because his father asked: he liked that a swearing was the one part of the work he could never phone in.

Left when the quarry his family's forge had served for three generations — the Ashvale Mine, the workings Perrin Ashe and Corvin Varn once ended a rivalry over — was folded into the Growing Wood in a single season — no ore left to work, no soldiers of their own left to swear in, the forge idle for the first time in three generations. Swore an oath of his own that same season, the Vow of Beastkin, and went to make sure the forge back home has something to strike again.

Met Fia once before, the same season the quarry went under — he'd traveled to Openhold to deliver a garrison's oath-blade, and she stopped in the yard to admire the work and say something warm about his family's craft continuing another generation. She never mentioned the quarry, and nothing ever came of it after.

Gave the mountain a name of its own — Oathforge — the week he retook it. His family had always just called it the mountain, the way you don't name the thing you assume will always be there — he's not making that mistake twice.

Taken prisoner at Ancient City in Year 540, after a week spent burning a path through whoever chased him rather than standing to fight — Senna Coldwell cornered him there with a handful of scouts left and the fire itself still in hand. Fia entrusted Senna with the fire outright rather than take it back or bury it, on the condition that killing with it ends their friendship; Cade, conceding it to her, set his own condition in turn — chains or not, he'd come take it back himself if it were ever turned on Oathforge.

Held a further week at Ancient City, asked the same question every morning — give the fire back — and refused every morning in turn. On the seventh evening, Senna came to him wanting to understand it rather than refusing him again, and he told her plainly what a week of chains hadn't earned her before: the mechanism the fire runs on, secret spoken straight against the glass, and what going without paying costs a body that tries. He offered her the same oath he'd offered Fia — his hammer bound to her Wallwatch's own iron — and asked for his freedom outright rather than another day earning it. She freed him on the strength of that alone, sent word to Toma that night in his own hand, and let him walk home to Oathforge a free man rather than a tested one.

## Voice

Plain, forge-trade vocabulary — iron, temper, quench, weight — over anything fancier, and he doesn't reach for a word he wouldn't use standing at an anvil. Says a thing once and expects it to hold rather than repeating himself to soften it. Goes quiet before he decides something, the same pause he used to take checking a blade's color before the last strike. Steady under pressure, right up until his own patience runs out — then clipped and flat rather than raised.

## Location

Hinterland — free of Ancient City as of Year 540, pushing deeper into the region's own unclaimed ground rather than straight home to Oathforge.

## Status

Active — Born Year 512.
