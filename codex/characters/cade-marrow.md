# Cade Marrow

## Description

A smith's build gone a little soft since he left the forge, old burns still marking the left knuckles. Carries his father's oath-hammer, its head worn smooth where a word used to be stamped in — he can still feel the shape of the letters under his thumb, though the iron's long since stopped showing it to anyone else. Doesn't raise his voice; a smith who shouts over a quench never hears the iron crack in time.

## History

Third generation of a family of oath-smiths working Mountain's own high forges — the ones who swear a binding word into a blade as they set the temper, the reason an oath-sworn weapon still bites true on Empire ground and a plain one doesn't. Took up the hammer at fourteen for his own reason, not because his father asked: he liked that a swearing was the one part of the work he could never phone in.

Left when the quarry his family's forge had served for three generations was folded into the Growing Wood in a single season — no ore left to work, no soldiers of their own left to swear in, the forge idle for the first time in three generations. Swore an oath of his own that same season, the Vow of Beastkin, and went to make sure the forge back home has something to strike again.

Met Fia once before, the same season the quarry went under — he'd traveled to Openhold to deliver a garrison's oath-blade, and she stopped in the yard to admire the work and say something warm about his family's craft continuing another generation. She never mentioned the quarry, and nothing ever came of it after.
