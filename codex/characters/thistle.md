# Thistle

## Description

Antlered, sharp-eyed, fae in the way of things that were never quite born so much as they arrived — she measures a person by whether they flinched first, not by rank or station. Carries nothing anyone's seen as a weapon, and doesn't seem to need one.

## History

One of three who answered when the ground itself woke at the edge of the Provinces this year, drawn by whatever the Emissary's own hand stirred loose there. She named herself rival to a khan nobody in Senna's household has ever heard of, and made it plain from the first that she meant to be paid back in a fight eventually. Senna took the price seriously enough to actually pay it — a secret handed over and a favor burned besides — rather than let her walk on with the other two.

## Location

Wastes

## Voice

Vocabulary level: blunt, almost formal — challenge and boast are the only two registers she seems to use. Verbal habit: answers with what she intends to do, never with what she thinks. Speech rhythm: short declaratives, no hedging, no trailing off.
