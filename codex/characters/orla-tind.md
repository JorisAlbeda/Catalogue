# Orla Tind

## Description

An Ashvale woman of about fifty who has spent most of her adult life saying in public what her neighbours only say indoors: that the Growing Wood is a conquest wearing a season's clothes, and that a border which moves is a border someone is moving. Three towns have asked her to leave over it, the last one in writing. Travels with a satchel of those letters and produces them the way another woman might produce references.

## History

Came up the valley road to Fertile Valley in Year 540 with the crowds thanking Cade Marrow for taking Ashvale's capital back off the bandits, and stayed behind after they had gone to tell him the thanking was early. Her argument was that a freed capital is worth nothing if whoever holds it begins behaving like whoever held it before. He agreed with her out loud, which she had not planned for, and kept her at his side rather than sending her on — an arrangement she has not entirely stopped being suspicious of.

## Location

Traveling with Cade Marrow, Hinterland

## Voice

Argues in questions she already knows the answer to, then waits out the silence instead of filling it. Reaches for the plain word wherever a softer one is offered — takings, not tribute; conquest, not growth. Has never once called Cade "lord" and shows no sign of beginning.
