# Hetty Doyle

**Description:** A brisk, ledger-quick trader who's run the stalls at Tinker's Fair since Ede Oswick, Perrine's own mother, first showed her how to balance a scale honestly two decades back. Keeps three different currencies sorted by ear before she's even looked at them, and never quotes a price twice if the first one didn't land.

**History:** Hetty apprenticed at Tinker's Fair under a cloth-trader from Ashvale who'd married into Openhold two generations back, and inherited the stall outright when the old woman's hands got too stiff to weigh anything fairly anymore. She was already running it alone by the time Ede Oswick arrived, newly out from her own family's mill and green at the actual arithmetic of trading fair — Hetty taught her the counting, and got taught back, by her own account, a patience she didn't have before. Twenty years on, she still credits Ede for that, and Ede's own daughter for never once needing the lesson repeated.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Voice:** Vocabulary level: plain, transactional. Verbal habit: names an exact number before she'll discuss anything else ("Six. Now what did you want to say?"). Emotional default: brisk, unbothered. Speech rhythm: clipped, fast.
