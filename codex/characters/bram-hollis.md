# Bram Hollis

## Description

Bram Hollis holds court in a Great Slum tavern that reeks of ale long before noon, one finger always aimed at somebody — a tax collector's manner without a tax collector's authority. He's never held office and never will, but he's the first to notice when someone new has, and the first to say so loudly enough for the whole room to hear it. He doesn't organize anything. He just makes sure nobody powerful gets to feel comfortable about it in his tavern.

## History

Rowan Voss met Bram and his circle of naysayers in Round 2, the same day she brought her company into the Great Slum looking for ground nobody else had claimed. He didn't ask to join anything — he just kept talking, the way he always does, and she let him keep doing it under her own banner instead of someone else's.
