# Doran Krell

## Description

Doran Krell is a relic-hunter who works the broken ground around Buried
Giant, bald and heavy-set, a thick black mustache he keeps waxed even on
campaign. He carries two curved blades and is never seen wearing both at
once — one stays sheathed, "for later." He sells to whoever's paying
this season and has never once lost a relic he'd decided he wanted.

## History

Recruited by Iona Ashe as her starting adviser, Round 0, at Buried
Giant. Revealed alongside her muster at Buried Giant, Round 0, the
same day the Great Spire's guards doubled. Round 3: brought Iona word
of the Spire's loss before the sun was up, flat and unsoftened —
already asking after what the Chancellor paid to turn her people
before she'd finished asking him to.
