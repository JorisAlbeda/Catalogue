# Fenn Larke

## Description

A wiry trail-guide who reads a track the way most people read a signpost — crouches at every crossing to check what's actually walked through recently, rather than trust what the road claims. Travels lighter than anyone Marek's ever paid, and always seems to already know which way the ground wants to go before anyone thinks to ask him.

## History

First guided one of Marek's caravans through backcountry no cart route was ever meant to survive, two years back, saving the crew days off a run that should have cost a week and a fair count of coin besides. Marek's kept him on retainer ever since — less out of loyalty than out of knowing exactly what a shortcut like that is worth. Kept his own name off any manifest the whole time, by his own choice, until Marek finally put a real name to what had been just "the guide" for two years running.

## Location

Threadbare, Provinces — traveling with Marek Sten.

## Voice

Says little, and almost none of it about people — distances, water, what's been through a place lately, when to trust a trail and when not to. Answers a direct question with exactly as many words as it needs and not one more.
