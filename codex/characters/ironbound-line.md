# Ironbound Line

## Description

Three warbands strong, all of them former forge-hands from Oathforge's own high furnaces — strikers, coal-boys, and one journeyman who never finished her own apprenticeship once the quarry went quiet. None of them soldiers by trade; they picked up a spear the same season they set down a hammer, because an idle forge doesn't feed anyone and Cade Marrow was the only one offering a wage that wasn't a Bandit's cut. Led by Sera Hollow, who ran the forge's own night shift before it went dark and runs this the same way — short on patience, shorter on excuses.

## History

Raised the season the Ashvale Mine below the forge was folded into the Growing Wood, from workers with nothing left to strike iron against. Marched with Cade Marrow when he swore his own oath and left to make the Empire answer for it, and returned with him to the mountain itself the day he moved to take his family's own ground back from the men squatting on it.

Took the yard back at a cost — Corin Tass, one of the original three, given up to force the fight. Two remain, standing at Oathforge now as its own garrison rather than travelling on with Cade.

## Location

Oathforge, Hinterland

## Voice

Sera Hollow speaks for the line in short, work-floor orders — phrased the same way she once called out a batch to the next hand down the row. Gives the instruction before the reason, and only adds the reason at all if someone's slow to move. Even-tempered by habit, not patience — the same evenness a forge demands of anyone who works too close to it.
