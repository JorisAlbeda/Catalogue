# Sammy

## Description

A Giant Python, appearing as a feral snake from hell to most who meet him, yet docile and even affectionate toward those he trusts — he enjoys being scratched on the head, which calms his otherwise fierce demeanor. Old in a way that doesn't map to a human span — old enough to have served more than one empire, and outlasted all of them.

Wears three shapes, not two. A traveler's shape first — a man like any other, unremarkable enough to hold a first drink at a bar without a second look, which is the only one most inns or empires ever get to know him by. His true length second — considerable, scaled, older than the walls of whatever keep happens to be standing that century. And between the two, rarest of the three: waist-up a man, waist-down a coiled serpent's tail, scales climbing his throat and forearms, tongue forking when he forgets himself mid-sentence, eyes gone to slits. He holds that middle shape only for whoever he's decided has earned the truth of him outright — no empire he's ever served, the Woodland Empire included, got shown that one.

## History

Recruited in the third month of an earlier war, joining Lilianne's rangers at Narrow Pass. His docile nature toward her and her team made him a valuable companion despite his fearsome appearance, valued more for intimidation and morale than for fighting outright. He let some of them travel on his own body, carrying them great distances — carried Lilianne and Naiad all the way from the Cradle to the Hinterland, where they met with Perrin. Later carried Lilith Woodborne to Openhold and back to the Deep Woods, docile as ever, already older than any throne he'd served.

Turned up at Farrin Dale's own inn at Salt Flats generations later, in Teagan Sedge's own time, drawn in by the same fiddle music and full bellies as everyone else who's started wandering that way. Told her plainly that he'd served an empire, now long gone, without volunteering which one or how long ago it fell — she didn't press him on it. Took her offer and stayed.

Showed her the middle shape their first evening outside, at the old well — not the full length, not the traveler's face, but the one in between. Whatever that's worth, she hasn't said, and he hasn't asked.

A week later, found Tinney Woodborne already at Farrin's table — kin, by the sound of it, to the same Lilith Woodborne he once carried to Openhold and back. Traded a story about Lilianne's own hand for Tinney's own delight at the connection, and was still at that table when the sky went green over Buried Giant. Recognized the color before either of the others said its name; he'd seen it twice before, neither time kindly.

Decades passed at Salt Flats the way they always do for him — unhurried, unmarked on him at all. Outlasted Teagan's own working years the same way he'd outlasted every empire before hers, still at Farrin's table some nights, still not saying which empire he served first.

## Location

Salt Flats, Provinces

## Status

Active — unchanged by the years, as ever.

## Voice

Vocabulary level: unhurried and concrete — reaches for one specific memory rather than a general feeling, and lets a long pause sit before he gets to it. Verbal habit: measures spans of time against empires and centuries rather than years, the way a shorter-lived listener would measure them against seasons; turns a big question back into one small, physical detail (a hand, a scratch, a particular cup) instead of answering it in the abstract. Emotional default: settled, not performed — grief for him reads as familiarity kept rather than wound reopened. Speech rhythm: unbothered, storyteller's pacing, willing to let a joke land before the weight underneath it does.
