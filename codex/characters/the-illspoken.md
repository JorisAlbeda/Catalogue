# The Illspoken

## Description

Three warbands raised out of Oathforge's own forge-town on a call put out in Toma Marrow's name rather than his son's. Carters, tally-keepers, publicans and the wives of men the quarry killed — people who kept accounts of who owed what to whom through three years of the forge standing idle, and who were never shy about reading those accounts aloud in the street. They are not soldiers and do not pretend to be. What they are is a company that arrives somewhere already knowing everybody's business. Led by Marra Skeel, who kept the forge's own debt-book before there was nothing left to write in it.

## History

Raised at Oathforge in Year 540, the first time Cade Marrow had climbed the switchback road since driving the squatters out of his family's yard. He put the call out under his father's name and it was answered inside a week, largely by people who had been waiting three years for the forge to ask them for something.

## Location

Traveling with Cade Marrow, Hinterland

## Voice

Marra Skeel delivers bad news in the same tone as the weather and keeps a running tally of every favour the company is owed by anyone it has ever helped. Prefaces roughly half of what she says with "now, I'm not one to talk," and then talks.
