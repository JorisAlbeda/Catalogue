# The Spearbound

## Description

Openhold's own Wallwatch companies, carried on the Truthwatcher's own board rather than posted at any gate — eight companies as of the fifth month of 540, oath-sworn at the collar as every blade at Openhold's wall is, and since this season carrying iron set with a Hinterland smith's word instead of plain steel. Tavin Aldren leads them, and reports a count before he reports a reason.

## History

Raised by Senna Coldwell out of what was left of the old gate-guard the year she finally claimed the Wallwatch's own command openly, and drilled to her own pattern instead of the one Dunstan Reeve left behind. It has been broken and remade twice since, both times on whatever ground it happened to be standing on. Four companies went into the Widowmire mud inside a single month against Farrowhold's guns — the better part of three hundred men, by Tavin's own count. The better part of eighty more burned on the road to Ancient City, chasing a man who never once stood to meet them.

Barely a third of what answers to that banner now marched out of Openhold under it: a dozen off Ancient City's own scattered folk, a dozen more of the Widowmire preaching crowd, and whoever of the original line is still walking. Whether a company built this fast out of strangers holds under a real push is a question nobody at the gate has asked out loud.

## Location

Widowmire, Provinces

## Status

Active — eight companies, under Tavin Aldren.
