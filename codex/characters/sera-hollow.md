# Sera Hollow

## Description

Leads the Ironbound Line, the three warbands Cade Marrow keeps on his own board rather than at any site — quarry hands and forge guards before they were anything else, used to holding a line on a slope that won't forgive a bad step. Sera drills them the way she'd temper a blade: slow, deliberate, no wasted motion, and she trusts a read of the ground over a shouted order.

## History

Ran the watch on the quarry road below Oathforge for a decade, back when the quarry still answered to Cade's own family forge — knew every switchback on it well enough to walk blind, which is more or less what the Line still does after dark. Signed on with Cade the same season he swore the Vow of Beastkin and set out to answer for the quarry's loss properly, on the strength of years working the same ground rather than any coin changing hands; she remembers exactly who paid what they owed the forge and who never did.

## Location

Oathforge, Hinterland
