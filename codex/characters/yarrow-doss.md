# Yarrow Doss

## Description

Yarrow Doss is a stooped, soft-spoken collector of songs nobody else
wanted, a cracked lute case slung over one shoulder more for show than
use — he hasn't restrung it in years. A fixture at the same Ashvale
hearth Iona Ashe's coat has hung over for generations, older than Wynn
by a good deal more than one, he's traded a song or a rumor for a drink
there longer than Wynn's been alive, and swears every song worth
knowing got thrown out by somebody first before somebody else picked it
back up.

## History

An old regular of the tavern where Iona Ashe's coat has hung for
generations, Yarrow offered to come along when Wynn set out for Buried
Giant, Round 0, to see the fallen Spire for himself — recruited as his
adviser there, drawn by the same page of old songs he's heard sung at
that hearth for years.

## Location

Buried Giant

## Voice

Vocabulary simple, laced with half-remembered lyric fragments; verbal
habit of answering a direct question with a scrap of an old song
instead of a straight answer; emotional default wry and unbothered;
speech rhythm rambling.
