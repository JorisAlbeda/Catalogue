# Sela Brand

## Description

A broker in the loosest sense of the word — deals in salt, secrets, or a friendly face, whichever a newcomer actually needs. Keeps a battered tin box of coin sample-weights on a strap across one shoulder, more habit than necessity, and looks over anyone new to Salt Flats twice before naming a price. Smiles easily, but never before she's decided what you're worth to her.

## History

First appeared the year Teagan Sedge marched on Salt Flats — the first person Teagan found already working the bandit-held ground when she arrived meaning to reclaim what trade she still could there. Stayed on afterward as the site's own broker rather than following Teagan anywhere else — this ground is her trade, not any one ruler of it.

Decades on, still the one who names the price first — older, slower on her feet, but she's never once let that stop her looking a newcomer over twice before naming what they're worth to her.

## Location

Farrowhold, Provinces

## Status

Active — old now, still working the same stall out of habit more than need.

## Voice

Vocabulary level: mixed — trade jargon dropped into otherwise plain talk. Verbal habit: names a price before a name ("Two favors first, then who are you"), and never repeats an offer twice. Emotional default: warmly transactional — she likes people fine, once they've paid for the privilege. Speech rhythm: clipped, no wasted words.
