# Cobb Stane

## Description

Cobb Stane keeps the boundary stones at Charming Valley — waist-high markers chiseled with old pacts, half of them worn unreadable by two centuries of rain. He believes a pact stops being real the moment nobody can still read it. Once a season he holds what the valley calls the Burning: every claimant brings their proofs to his fire, and he'll let each of them keep exactly one. He carries a chisel he's never explained and won't let anyone else touch.

## History

Cobb Stane has kept Charming Valley's boundary stones for longer than anyone currently living can confirm; the Truthwatcher's desk changed hands so many times he stopped learning each new claimant's face, judging them instead by whether their proof survives the Burning. First appeared, Round 0, taken on as Marren Wick's adviser.
