# Fenna Rook

## Description

Fenna Rook stands rooted at Buried Giant, bark climbed past both wrists now and up one side of her jaw, grey shawl gone to grey-green lichen at the shoulders — the shape of the woman who once warded graves and boundary-stones for a living still readable in what's left of her stance and her face. Still sharp about manners, the kind owed to the dead and to whatever they left behind; still corrects anyone who calls what she does magic. Doesn't warn people off a line anymore so much as see past it — asked right, she can draw a Vision up out of the world itself, the same instinct that once told her where a grave-robber shouldn't dig.

## History

Fenna spent years warding the quiet, unglamorous graves nobody paid much attention to around Ashvale — a boundary-stone here, a family plot there, keeping the desperate and the greedy alike from digging where they shouldn't. Word travels among people in that trade, and word reached her fast when Buried Giant changed hands: a grave finally answering to someone who meant to guard it rather than loot it, for the first time anyone in her trade could remember. She walked in within the week and asked for work doing exactly what she already does.

Within her second month on that ground, something wearing a voice that wasn't its own walked into the Cairn Watch's camp at night and turned her into a tree in front of the whole company — smiling, apologetic, and entirely untroubled about it, gone again before anyone could get a straight answer out of whatever it actually was. It never came back to undo it, whether it forgot, couldn't, or simply moved on to whatever its own mission actually was. She's stood rooted at Buried Giant ever since, and Wynn Ashe has since found her changed shape good for more than mourning: reaching whatever's left of her wardswoman's own eye for the boundary between worlds, she can be asked to draw a Vision up out of the world itself, same as she once drew a line no grave-robber would cross.

## Location

Buried Giant, Hinterland

## Status

Transformed, not Deceased. Rooted in place at Buried Giant
since the second month of the war, changed by something that never
identified itself and never came back to undo it. Can't travel or ward
a line by hand anymore, but retains a working mind and her old
wardswoman's sense for a boundary, now turned toward drawing a Vision
up out of the world rather than warning grave-robbers off a plot.
Decades on, still rooted at the same spot, bark climbed further still
but the mind behind it no less sharp — whatever changed her shows no
sign of loosening its hold, for better or worse.

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain, precise about her own trade's terms (wards, lines, manners) and otherwise sparing. Verbal habit: corrects anyone who calls what she does magic, every time, without fail. Emotional default: quietly proprietary — she doesn't warm to a new employer so much as start treating their business as her own. Speech rhythm: unhurried, willing to let a question sit until she's decided how much of an answer it deserves.
