# Fenna Rook

## Description

Fenna Rook wards graves and boundary-stones for a living, or did, before there was a paying ground left in Ashvale that still needed it. Grey-shawled, soft-spoken, and never without a pouch of chalk and iron filings at her belt for marking a line she means to hold. Doesn't call what she does magic, exactly — calls it manners, the kind owed to the dead and to whatever they left behind, and gets sharp with anyone who treats either carelessly.

## History

Fenna spent years warding the quiet, unglamorous graves nobody paid much attention to around Ashvale — a boundary-stone here, a family plot there, keeping the desperate and the greedy alike from digging where they shouldn't. Word travels among people in that trade, and word reached her fast when Buried Giant changed hands: a grave finally answering to someone who meant to guard it rather than loot it, for the first time anyone in her trade could remember. She walked in within the week and asked for work doing exactly what she already does.

## Location

Buried Giant, Hinterland

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain, precise about her own trade's terms (wards, lines, manners) and otherwise sparing. Verbal habit: corrects anyone who calls what she does magic, every time, without fail. Emotional default: quietly proprietary — she doesn't warm to a new employer so much as start treating their business as her own. Speech rhythm: unhurried, willing to let a question sit until she's decided how much of an answer it deserves.
