# Petra Vane

**Description:** A wiry courier who trades in safe-passage signals rather than goods — whistle-calls, cairns, a particular knot tied at a particular height, the kind of language that tells a traveler which stretch of a bandit-held road is quiet this week. Carries no visible weapon and doesn't need one; word is the bandit camps let her through because she's never once carried anything worth robbing her for, and never once lied about what she saw.

**History:** Petra grew up in a family of Provinces salt-traders who lost their own route the year the bandit clans took Salt Flats outright, and rather than give up the road entirely, she learned to read it on the bandits' own terms instead — which whistle-call means safe passage, which cairn means turn back. She's carried messages and small cargo for anyone willing to pay, bandit and trader alike, for the better part of twenty years now, trusted on both sides of that line for the same reason: she's never once carried anything worth robbing her for, and never once lied about what she saw. When Ossian Fenwick went asking around Openhold's own social circles for someone who'd actually run that ground rather than crossed it once, hers was the name that kept coming up.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Voice:** Vocabulary level: plain, economical — trims a sentence to whatever a traveler actually needs to hear. Verbal habit: describes distance in signals and landmarks rather than miles ("past the third cairn, where the whistle changes"). Emotional default: unbothered, faintly amused by anyone in a hurry. Speech rhythm: clipped, but never rushed.
