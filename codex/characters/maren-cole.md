# Maren Cole

## Description

Maren Cole was a Truthwatcher, one of the order Wren Sable founded to hold the Sorcerer's old promise of a kinder, truer world. She kept the order's vow literally: no coin passed through her hands that she hadn't earned by the day's end, and she'd sooner go without than take a favor she hadn't asked for outright. Plainly dressed, sharp-eyed, and unbothered by silence, she measured people by what they were willing to do without.

## History

Maren attached herself to Rowan Voss's company as it left Ashvale, on no one's authority but her own. She said only that the Truthwatchers had heard the same rumors of a new war brewing in the Woodland Empire that everyone else had, and that a vow of poverty tends to see more clearly than a full purse does.
