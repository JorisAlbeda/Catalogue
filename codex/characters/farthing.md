# Farthing

**Description:** A rangy grey-and-cream gelding, narrow through the chest and built for distance rather than show — the kind of horse a courier keeps, not a parade mount. Ears flick toward road-noise before his rider even hears it. Answers to a whistle, not a rein-tug, a habit from whoever trained him first.

**History:** Bred and broken on one of the tally-circuit farms east of the Openhold Plains, the same stock that's supplied Openhold's own couriers for two generations — fast over a short haul, steady enough not to spook at a bandit-country river crossing. He ran a courier's circuit for six years under a rider who named him for the smallest coin a courier carries, on the theory that a courier's horse should be worth exactly what it does and not a coin more. That rider aged out of the saddle this spring and passed him along rather than put him out to pasture; Perrine took him on the same week she heard the name and decided she liked the theory behind it.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Voice:** N/A — a horse. Reads as watchful and unbothered rather than skittish; the closest thing to a "verbal habit" is the ear-flick that gives away trouble on a road before anyone mounted notices it themselves.
