# Bracken Sorel

**Description:** A weathered beast-handler who trades in hides, antlers, and half-tamed hunting stock brought in from beyond Openhold's own walls — one of the only people at Plains who still regularly goes past the mapped roads and comes back. Keeps a huntsman's whistle on a cord at his throat, worn smooth, and smells permanently of woodsmoke and wet fur.

**History:** Bracken grew up the youngest of a family that's kept the beast-pens at Plains for three generations, but broke from that trade at seventeen to run three seasons with a hunting company working the country past Salt Flats, trading pelts to whoever would still take them before the roads turned too dangerous even for that. He came back older than three seasons should have made him, sold most of what he'd learned as stories rather than goods, and settled back into his family's own stall rather than leave again. Anyone who asks him the right question still gets the real answer, though most people at Plains only ever ask about the pelts.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Voice:** Vocabulary level: simple, plain. Verbal habit: answers a question about the wider country before the one he was actually asked ("You're asking about coin. I'll tell you what I saw past the second treeline first."). Emotional default: watchful, faintly restless. Speech rhythm: slow, deliberate.
