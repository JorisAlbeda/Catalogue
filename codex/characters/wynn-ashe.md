# Wynn Ashe

## Description

Wynn Ashe is lean and quiet-handed, chalk dust worked permanently into
the seams of a plain travelling coat, a half-worn stone chisel through
his belt more out of habit now than use. He carries a single folded
page, ink gone soft at the creases, in the same inside pocket at all
times, and counts a debt unsettled the same way the old family stories
say Iona Ashe always did — a debt eighty years old now, and by his own
reckoning still not paid.

## History

Wynn grew up in an Ashvale tavern his own family has kept for
generations, Iona Ashe's coat hanging by its hearth since long before
his grandmother's time, the tavern-song page still sewn into its
lining — several generations separate him from Iona herself, who died
long before he was born, but the family kept both the coat and her old
habit of never calling a debt settled just because it's gone quiet.
His branch has sent someone out to check on the Great Spire's memorial
stone every so often since Iona first asked for its recutting, eighty
years back; three years ago, at sixteen and newly apprenticed to an
Ashvale mason, it was Wynn's own turn, and he spent that summer
re-tracing the worn grooves of Ressa Kindler's own words to keep them
legible — upkeep, not authorship. The Spire was never just a monument to his family — it marked the
ground where the Darkest Flame itself was buried, and the tending
visits were never really about the inscription. Word reached the
tavern only this past season that the tower had come down entirely,
filed by Openhold's own reports as nothing but bad weather, no one's
ledger to answer for. What frightened Wynn wasn't the ruling — it was
that Buried Giant has sat in bandit hands for years regardless, picked
over by relic-hunters like Sorren Krell hoping for exactly the kind of
find that shouldn't be found, with nothing left standing now to mark
what's actually underfoot. His own ancestor Iona tried to take this
same ground once, for the same reason, and lost it back to the
Woodland Empire before she could hold it. Wynn petitioned Openhold for
leave to raise a company of Hold volunteers — mostly Ashvale folk who
know the old stories as well as he does — and marched them to Buried
Giant himself, at nineteen, taking a copy of the coat's own song and
the tavern's own Yarrow Doss along with him. Arrived Round 0, and
recruited Yarrow as his adviser there, meaning to hold this ground
outright rather than watch it from a tavern hearth three hundred miles
off.

## Voice

Plain and precise, a tradesman's vocabulary rather than a scholar's —
measures and materials over abstractions, though he reads well enough
to quote the old page word for word when it matters. Verbal habit:
answers a direct question by citing the page or "what's written" before
ever giving his own opinion first, and talks about danger and trust the
same way a ledger talks about debts — settled, unsettled, owed.
Emotional default: quietly urgent, a mason's patience on the surface
over something coiled tight underneath — he goes stiller and more
clipped exactly when he's most afraid, not louder. Speech rhythm:
measured and clipped, short considered sentences, with a slight pause
before answering, as if checking his own words against the page first.
