# The Old Oak

## Description

A single rooted sprig no longer than a forearm, cut green and somehow never drying — bark still soft enough to dent with a thumbnail, three leaves that haven't browned since the day it came into her keeping. It doesn't sit still the way a cut branch should; turn it toward the window overnight and it's found true north again by morning, slow as anything grows, but moved all the same.

## History

Not carried out of the Second Woodland Empire's own deep growth by choice — offered, at the edge of Openhold's own fields, by nothing Senna could ever afterward describe as a person. Since the wood past the border became, by every account she trusts, one and the same as its own Emissary, she keeps the sprig less as a trading favor and more as an open line she never asked for: whatever Heart of the Oak wants known reaches her through it before it reaches her through any rider. She trades with it plainly, the way the desk requires of her, and has stopped pretending to herself the trade only ever runs one direction.

## Location

Openhold — carried with Senna Coldwell wherever she goes.

## Voice

It doesn't speak. What it does instead: leans a leaf toward whatever it wants noticed, sheds one when a trade displeases it, and once — only once so far — bent flat overnight toward Heart of the Oak the same morning word reached Openhold that the border had moved again. Senna's stopped calling that a coincidence.
