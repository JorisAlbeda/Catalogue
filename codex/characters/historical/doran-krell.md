# Doran Krell

## Description

Doran Krell was a relic-hunter who worked the broken ground around Buried
Giant, bald and heavy-set, a thick black mustache he kept waxed even on
campaign. He carried two curved blades and was never seen wearing both at
once — one stayed sheathed, "for later." He sold to whoever was paying
that season and never once lost a relic he'd decided he wanted.

## History

Recruited by Iona Ashe as her starting adviser at Buried
Giant, revealed alongside her muster there the same day the Great Spire's guards doubled. Later in that campaign, he brought Iona word
of the Spire's loss before the sun was up, flat and unsoftened —
already asking after what the Chancellor paid to turn her people
before she'd finished asking him to.

## Location

Buried Giant

## Status

Deceased — long since; whatever finally caught up with a man who made his living wanting other people's relics went unrecorded.
