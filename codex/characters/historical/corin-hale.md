# Corin Hale

## Description

An old drillmaster gone grey on the road, more often found sitting with the wolf that trailed him than with the soldiers he'd come to see. Carried no weapon worth the name — just a battered oath-book, and the wolf, which sat when he sat and rose when he rose, and never once had to be called.

## History

Corin spent decades drilling garrisons that answered to the Hold in name only — scattered, half-forgotten postings that never once mustered together, let alone fought together. He took to walking between them himself, unpaid and mostly unasked, reminding each one that the company at the next post over answered to the same banner and owed them the same debt in a fight. Most garrisons humored him. A few actually held to it. He turned up at Buried Giant's own gate not long after the bandit banner came down, looked over the ground a while, and told Wynn Ashe plainly that a company this size, holding ground this important, ought to know it isn't alone — wherever else the Hold's own warbands were standing, they were his to call on too, if he only asked it right. Stayed on afterward, still walking the same argument between whatever garrisons would listen, until his legs stopped letting him. Died at Buried Giant, Year 505, the wolf gone quiet at his side before he was.

## Location

Buried Giant, Hinterland

## Status

Deceased — died at Buried Giant, Year 505, old age.

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain, soldier's shorthand — companies, postings, musters, never anything grander. Verbal habit: addressed the wolf mid-sentence, half to it and half to whoever he was actually talking to, as if it understood the point better than the person did. Emotional default: patient past the point most people would call it stubbornness — he'd been ignored by better men than whoever was listening now. Speech rhythm: slow, repetitive, willing to say the same thing three ways until it landed.
