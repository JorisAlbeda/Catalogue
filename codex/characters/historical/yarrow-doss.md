# Yarrow Doss

## Description

Yarrow Doss was a stooped, soft-spoken collector of songs nobody else
wanted, a cracked lute case slung over one shoulder more for show than
use — he hadn't restrung it in years. A fixture at the same Ashvale
hearth Iona Ashe's coat hung over for generations, older than Wynn by a
good deal more than one, he'd traded a song or a rumor for a drink
there longer than Wynn had been alive, and swore every song worth
knowing got thrown out by somebody first before somebody else picked it
back up.

## History

An old regular of the tavern where Iona Ashe's coat hung for
generations, Yarrow offered to come along when Wynn set out for Buried
Giant, Year 492, to see the fallen Spire for himself — recruited as his
adviser there, drawn by the same page of old songs he'd heard sung at
that hearth for years. Stayed at Buried Giant through everything that
followed, still at Wynn's table the night Teagan Sedge came to name
what Wynn was. Told an old, unfinished story that night about a smith
who never asked who his blades found, and admitted he didn't remember
how it ended. He finished it before he died — Buried Giant's own
watch still half-remembers a verse or two, though none of them agree
on the words.

## Location

Buried Giant

## Status

Deceased — died at Buried Giant, Year 501, old age.

## Voice

Vocabulary simple, laced with half-remembered lyric fragments; verbal
habit of answering a direct question with a scrap of an old song
instead of a straight answer; emotional default wry and unbothered;
speech rhythm rambling.
