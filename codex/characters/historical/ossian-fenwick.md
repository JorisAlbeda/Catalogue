# Ossian Fenwick

**Description:** A stooped, owlish old road-warden, wrapped in a faded blue travel-cloak he'd had re-stitched more times than he'd admit. Carried a single walking staff worn smooth at the grip, and always seemed to be listening for something past the treeline before he answered. Didn't hurry, on the road or in conversation.

**History:** Ossian spent nearly forty years walking the roads between Cradle's outlying farms as an unofficial toll-warden, long before anyone at the desk was young enough to notice him — guiding traders around bandit patrols for whatever they could spare, and keeping no record of his own beyond what his memory held. He buried a wife at Salt Flats the year before it fell outright to the bandit clans, and never went back, though he'd never have said so outright if asked. When word went round that the newly-seated Truthwatcher wanted someone who actually knew the back roads rather than just the maps of them, half of the Openhold Plains gave her his name before she'd finished asking.

He outlived Perrine Oswick by only a handful of seasons, still walking the roads he'd always walked, and died in his own bed outside the Openhold Plains — the same quiet way he'd lived, no ledger entry needed to say it happened.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Status:** Deceased — old age, Openhold Plains, Year 499 of the Old Oak.

**Voice:** Plainspoken, no scholarly flourishes. Verbal habit: answers most questions with a specific memory of a specific stretch of road ("There's a bend past the third ford where the Bandits never bother looking") rather than a direct opinion, and measures distance in walking-time, never miles. Emotional default: unhurried and watchful. Speech rhythm: measured, with a pause before anything that matters.
