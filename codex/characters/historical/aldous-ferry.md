# Aldous Ferry

## Description

Aldous Ferry rode escort on Perrine Oswick's own tally circuit for the better part of three years before she ever took the desk — the one who actually carried a blade on those boundary disputes, while she carried the cord. Broad-shouldered, quick to laugh, and visibly still getting used to being saluted; commands what people at the Openhold Plains have started calling the Tally Company, after the trade its own captain and its own Truthwatcher share.

## History

Aldous grew up the son of an Openhold Plains cooper, apprenticed to the trade himself for two years before deciding he was better with a spear than a stave, and hired on with a small circuit-escort outfit that happened to be guarding Perrine Oswick's own rounds the year he turned nineteen. He liked her plain way of settling an argument well enough to keep re-signing every season after, long after the pay stopped mattering as much as the company did. When Perrine started actually recruiting a company of her own rather than just borrowing one, Aldous was the first name on the list, and the only one she didn't have to ask twice.

He led the Tally Company into the breach at Openhold Keep's own gate alongside Wynn Ashe's Cairn Watch, and didn't come back out of it — the company spent to the last warband taking a wall that had held against everything thrown at it before that day.

## Location

Plains, Cradle

## Status

Deceased — killed breaking Openhold Keep's gate
