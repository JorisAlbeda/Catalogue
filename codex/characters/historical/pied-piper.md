# Pied Piper

## Description

A separate account of the same non-event described under Dennet Fife: a figure briefly rumored to have been drawn toward Charming Valley and the Ruined Temple, tied to neither Perrin Ashe's line nor to Marren Wick's growing network, before the old law there turned back anyone else who reached for it.

## History

The two accounts don't fully agree — one names the figure outright as Dennet Fife, the other simply as "the Pied Piper," and there's no telling now whether that's one man remembered two ways or two rumors that grew into each other over time. Either way, nothing was ever actually claimed: the Ruined Temple's own restriction held, and whoever or whatever this was left Charming Valley the same way it arrived, unrecorded.

**Editor's note:** See `dennet-fife.md`. We've kept both entries rather than guess which, if either, is the more accurate account.

Charming Valley
