# Corvin Varn

## Description

Corvin Varn was an exile with blue-grey skin, broad shoulders, and close-cropped hair that had turned prematurely grey at the temples. He wore a signet ring too large for his hand, which had been resized twice by another jeweler. His demeanor was deliberate and stubborn, treating every unanswered question as unfinished work. He avoided grief but refused to let debts remain unpaid, even when inconvenient. His presence carried a quiet intensity, often marked by a measured tone and a refusal to engage in unnecessary conflict.

## History

From the moment he reached adulthood, Corvin Varn was burdened by his inheritance: the unresolved death of his grandmother, Isolde Varn, who fell at Drowned City fighting the Sorcerer of the Woods generations before he was born.

He seized Fertile Valley and Boiling Lake from the bandits squatting there in the opening days of the war against Lilianne's Woodland Empire, only to lose both to Perrin Ashe within the week — the two of them rivals from the start, each treating the other as the obstacle standing between himself and the Empire. The rivalry ran skirmish after skirmish until it came to a head at what would later be called Ashvale Mine: a fight neither of them fully won, and both walked away from having decided the other wasn't worth losing more men to. They struck a truce there and held to it — not allies yet, just neutral to each other, each free to spend his own strength on Lilianne instead of on one another.

He raised his own army afterward and marched on the Woodland Empire in earnest, but was defeated by Lilianne outright — the same season Perrin's own campaign against her was gathering real strength. Rather than let that defeat be the end of it, he found a place in Perrin's own cause instead. Near the war's end, as Perrin moved to found Ashvale out of what the truce with the Empire finally yielded, whatever had cooled between them since the Mine warmed into something neither had planned on. They built Ashvale together after that in every sense — not just co-founders, but partners, seen together for the rest of the war and long past it.

With Ashvale founded and standing, he kept Ressa Kindler at his own table despite his family's old history of conflict with her clan, and it was through her — a Fire Talker whose own account long outlived her — that he finally learned the true circumstances of his grandmother's death: the third charge, the two the Fire Talkers had turned back before it. A year on, he left Ashvale and traveled to Drowned City himself to settle what the marker there had never said, resolving lingering questions about Isolde's own legacy, including her role in the exile of Wren Sable.
