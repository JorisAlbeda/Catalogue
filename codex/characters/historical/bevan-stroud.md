# Bevan Stroud

## Description

Bevan Stroud was a stooped, soft-spoken man who wore the same patched grey robe for the last twenty years of his life, remembered in Openhold less for anything he did than for a phrase he coined and the town still repeats: "the ledger records the world, it doesn't go looking for more of it." He kept every column balanced to the coin, and in thirty-one years at the desk never once approved a warband to cross Salt Flats.

## History

Bevan took up the Truthwatcher's seat at forty, the third to hold it since Marren Wick's founding generation, inheriting an Openhold already comfortable inside its own three sites and content to stay that way. He governed carefully and honestly — trade disputes settled fairly, the granary's own accounts never once found short under his watch — but treated the two unclaimed stretches past Salt Flats as a closed question rather than an open one, the same as the Truthwatchers before him had. He died of a winter fever three years ago, at seventy-one, still at his desk, the ledger open in front of him to a page recording that season's grain yield. Perrine Oswick was seated in his place after a season's search for a successor honest enough for the office and young enough to hold it a while.
