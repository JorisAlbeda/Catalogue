# Farthing

**Description:** A rangy grey-and-cream gelding, narrow through the chest and built for distance rather than show — the kind of horse a courier keeps, not a parade mount. His ears flicked toward road-noise before his rider ever heard it. Answered to a whistle, not a rein-tug, a habit from whoever trained him first.

**History:** Bred and broken on one of the tally-circuit farms east of the Openhold Plains, the same stock that had supplied Openhold's own couriers for two generations — fast over a short haul, steady enough not to spook at a bandit-country river crossing. He ran a courier's circuit for six years under a rider who named him for the smallest coin a courier carries, on the theory that a courier's horse should be worth exactly what it does and not a coin more. That rider aged out of the saddle the spring before Perrine took him on, and she'd decided she liked the theory behind his name the same week she heard it.

He outlived Perrine by a little over a decade, put out to pasture at the Openhold Plains once his legs gave out for anything faster than a walk, and died there, old, in a paddock a courier's family kept for exactly that purpose.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Status:** Deceased — old age, Openhold Plains, Year 508 of the Old Oak.

**Voice:** N/A — a horse. Reads as watchful and unbothered rather than skittish; the closest thing to a "verbal habit" is the ear-flick that gives away trouble on a road before anyone mounted notices it themselves.
