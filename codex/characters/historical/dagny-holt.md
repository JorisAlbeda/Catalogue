# Dagny Holt

## Description

A wiry, sun-cracked rider who spent more nights in the saddle than out of it, one ear always cocked for hoofbeats that weren't her own. Kept a horn slung across her back that she never once needed to blow twice.

## History

Rode patrol for a grain caravan out of Ashvale before word reached her that Buried Giant was hiring hands who meant to keep the ground rather than pass through it. Arrived within the week and took to riding the perimeter the same way she'd ridden escort — just for a company that finally stayed put. Kept riding it for decades after, training up whoever came along young enough to take the saddle from her, until she was the one training the trainers. Outlived Wynn Ashe by a few years, still riding the same perimeter she'd first ridden at nineteen. Died at Buried Giant, Year 525.

## Location

Buried Giant, Hinterland

## Status

Deceased — died at Buried Giant, Year 525, old age.

## Voice

Vocabulary level: plain, economical, mostly about ground and distance ("half a day out," "riding double"). Verbal habit: reported what she'd seen before anyone asked, unprompted, as if the telling itself was the job. Emotional default: alert, unbothered by threat itself, only by surprise. Speech rhythm: clipped, arrived at the point immediately.
