# Bram Hollis

## Description

Bram Hollis held court in a Great Slum tavern that reeked of ale long before noon, one finger always aimed at somebody — a tax collector's manner without a tax collector's authority. He never held office and never did, but he was the first to notice when someone new had, and the first to say so loudly enough for the whole room to hear it. He didn't organize anything. He just made sure nobody powerful got to feel comfortable about it in his tavern.

## History

Rowan Voss met Bram and his circle of naysayers the same day she first brought her company into the Great Slum looking for ground nobody else had claimed. He didn't ask to join anything — he just kept talking, the way he always did, and she let him keep doing it under her own banner instead of someone else's.

Whatever became of him after Rowan's own war ended, the Slum's own tavern-talk never bothered to record — a fitting enough end for a man who spent his life making noise about everyone else's business rather than his own.

## Location

Great Slum

## Status

Deceased — long since, cause and year both lost to the same tavern gossip he used to feed on.
