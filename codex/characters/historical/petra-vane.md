# Petra Vane

**Description:** A wiry courier who traded in safe-passage signals rather than goods — whistle-calls, cairns, a particular knot tied at a particular height, the kind of language that told a traveler which stretch of a bandit-held road was quiet that week. Carried no visible weapon and didn't need one; word was the bandit camps let her through because she'd never once carried anything worth robbing her for, and never once lied about what she saw.

**History:** Petra grew up in a family of Provinces salt-traders who lost their own route the year the bandit clans took Salt Flats outright, and rather than give up the road entirely, she learned to read it on the bandits' own terms instead — which whistle-call means safe passage, which cairn means turn back. She carried messages and small cargo for anyone willing to pay, bandit and trader alike, trusted on both sides of that line for the same reason: she'd never once carried anything worth robbing her for, and never once lied about what she saw. When Ossian Fenwick went asking around Openhold's own social circles for someone who'd actually run that ground rather than crossed it once, hers was the name that kept coming up.

She kept the roads long after Perrine's death, training two more couriers to read a whistle-call the same careful way before she finally set the signals down herself. Died at Salt Flats, old age, still trusted by both sides of the road she'd spent her whole life on.

**Location:** Plains, Cradle

**Status:** Deceased — old age, Salt Flats, Year 528 of the Old Oak.

**Voice:** Vocabulary level: plain, economical — trims a sentence to whatever a traveler actually needs to hear. Verbal habit: describes distance in signals and landmarks rather than miles ("past the third cairn, where the whistle changes"). Emotional default: unbothered, faintly amused by anyone in a hurry. Speech rhythm: clipped, but never rushed.
