# Corin Sale

## Description

A fast-talking broker out of the old Tinker's Fair circuit, more comfortable sealing a promise than a purse. Set up shop wherever there was room and a crowd wanting something, and had a habit of getting people to swear to things they'd rather not, worded so carefully they didn't notice until later.

## History

Placed by Iona Ashe at the Openhold Plains — planted in Lilith Woodborne's own dooryard on the strength of Bryn Holt's knack for finding room to grow things.

## Location

Plains

## Status

Deceased — long since. The kind of broker nobody misses out loud, whatever they owed him.
