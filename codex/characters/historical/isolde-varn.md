# Isolde Varn

## Description

Isolde Varn took up the Varn name as a young woman, carrying forward one half of a debt her own family never finished paying. She led the push against the Sorcerer of the Woods at Drowned City, and fell there — the third charge to fall, after the Fire Talkers repelled the first two. Her legacy lives on in a marker at Drowned City that names no one and apologizes to no one, and in the grandson who, generations later, went looking for what that silence was actually hiding.

## History

Isolde Varn led the charge against the Sorcerer of the Woods at Drowned City, where the Fire Talkers turned back two waves of her attack before the Sorcerer himself ended the third, killing her outright. Whatever she'd gone to Drowned City to settle died with her, unrecorded — the marker later raised there names no one and apologizes to no one, exactly as she left it.

It fell to her grandson, Corvin Varn, generations later, to go looking for the truth the marker wouldn't tell. See `corvin-varn.md`.
