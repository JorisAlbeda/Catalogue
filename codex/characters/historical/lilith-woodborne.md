# Lilith Woodborne

## Description

Lilith Woodborne was the Chancellor of the Woodland Empire since the 400th year of the Old Oak. Known for her straight nut-brown hair and her brazen personality.

## History

A more adapt wielder of the blade than of diplomatic know-how, Lilith was nonetheless loved by her people.
In the 410th year of the Old Oak, granted audience to Marren Wick, claimant to the vacant Truthwatcher's desk, at Magician's Code. When pressed on the unclosed century-old entry for Sorrel Wick's death, Lilith declared the Truthwatcher post closed outright rather than answer for it, and ended the audience.

Rode out to the Marshes on Sammy, her python, to confront Rowan Voss, Warden of Ashvale, over Ashvale's refusal to help defend the Empire against Marren Wick and Iona Ashe. Rowan turned the offer down flat; Lilith called her selfish to her face and rode off without waiting for a reply.

Eventuall, Rowan rode to the plains on her own accord. The two armies lay seige to the Usurper's Keep, which would later become the capital of Openhold. Just as Lilith's army breached the wall, the Great Spire lit up, and all were blinded by the light of the Darkest Flame. Marren, however, closed the tome of the Darkest Secret, calming the Flame. This was treated by all present as the sign that Marren was the rightful future ruler. This marked the disbanding of the Woodland Empire.

Lilith lived out the rest of her days as the defender of the people of the Woodlands, which would be remembered as a quiet and mysterious place centred around the Old Oak.
