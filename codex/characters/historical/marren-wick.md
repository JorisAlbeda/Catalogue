# Marren Wick

## Description

Marren Wick is the newest claimant to the Truthwatcher's desk at Charming Valley, vacant since Sorrel Wick's exile a century ago. She keeps her hands open and visible whenever she speaks, deliberately, not out of habit. She wears a plain grey coat with brass buttons down the left side — nothing that draws the eye — and cropped her own hair short with a belt-knife the week she left the archive, rather than pin it back.

## History

Marren was born at Narrow Pass on the Ashvale side of the border, in Daz's household — the same place Sorrel actually lived out her exile and died, a hundred miles from any Empire ledger. She grew up hearing the true ending firsthand from people who'd known Sorrel in her last years, while the Empire's own rolls recorded nothing. That gap, between what Narrow Pass always knew and what the capital refused to write down, is what sent her looking for the desk in the first place.
Marren took the name Wick for herself when she took up the desk, since no confirmed death was ever entered against it in the Empire's rolls. She's since learned that gap was never a clerical oversight — Lilianne knew Sorrel died at Narrow Pass and chose not to close the entry, and every Chancellor since has left it that way. Marren means to close it herself, formally, from the desk Sorrel once held, whether or not the Woodland Empire's current line wants that question answered. She chalks the words of an old painting's title, *Neither Reaching Nor Kneeling*, inside her coat, and won't let an accusation finish before she's checked the accused's hands herself — a habit inherited from clerks who never met the woman whose name she borrowed.
Learning that the Darkest Secret is an actual tome — instructions for wielding the Flame itself, not just a title — confirmed what she'd already told Lilith to her face: the Woodborne line buries more than one thing the same way. Marren now means to take custody of it, not the throne it comes with; the Circlet of Command guarding it was itself pried from the Flame's own grave at the Great Spire, and taking it back reads to her as the same theft run a second time, this time by someone who means to open what it's hiding rather than keep it shut. She isn't planning to be the one who draws first — she means to let Iona Ashe's coming clash with Lilith at the Great Spire cost the Chancellor something before she moves.
Iona's clash didn't cost Lilith enough, so Marren stopped waiting. She marched on Plains herself, broke the Chancellor's last guards there, took the ring, and with it the tome it had been sealing shut. She has the Darkest Secret in hand now, not just a claim on it — and knows holding it makes her the next mark for every court watching the Provinces.

Marren held that ground rather than withdraw with her prize, named it Openhold, and sat its Truthwatcher's desk — the first this office ever owned outright — for decades afterward. She died there of old age, still at that same desk, some fifty-odd years before the current year of 492. The seat has passed through several Truthwatchers since, most recently Bevan Stroud, then Perrine Oswick.
