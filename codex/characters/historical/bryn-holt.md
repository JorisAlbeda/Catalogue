# Bryn Holt

## Description

A wiry forager who came out of the Spire's ruin with nothing broken and everything noticed, a rabbit-skin pouch of seeds and small stolen things slung at her hip. She had a knack for finding what was still willing to grow in ground everyone else had given up on, and never asked what a cause was called before deciding whether to help it take root.

## History

Recruited by Iona Ashe among the stragglers left behind after the Chancellor turned the crowd against her at the Great Spire, in the campaign's early going.

## Location

Buried Giant

## Status

Deceased — old age, long enough ago now that nobody at Buried Giant remembers the year, only that the ground kept whatever she'd planted there after she was gone.
