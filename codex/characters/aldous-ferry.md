# Aldous Ferry

## Description

Aldous Ferry rode escort on Perrine Oswick's own tally circuit for the better part of three years before she ever took the desk — the one who actually carried a blade on those boundary disputes, while she carried the cord. Broad-shouldered, quick to laugh, and visibly still getting used to being saluted; commands what people at Plains have started calling the Tally Company, after the trade its own captain and its own Truthwatcher share.

## History

Aldous grew up the son of a Plains cooper, apprenticed to the trade himself for two years before deciding he was better with a spear than a stave, and hired on with a small circuit-escort outfit that happened to be guarding Perrine Oswick's own rounds the year he turned nineteen. He liked her plain way of settling an argument well enough to keep re-signing every season after, long after the pay stopped mattering as much as the company did. When Perrine started actually recruiting a company of her own rather than just borrowing one, Aldous was the first name on the list, and the only one she didn't have to ask twice.
