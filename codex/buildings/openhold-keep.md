# Openhold Keep

## Description

An old, half-fallen tower on the open ground at Plains, older than anyone in Marren Wick's company could account for. Long squatted in by a bandit clan before Marren cleared and reclaimed it. The keep, and the town that grew up around it, gave the whole ground its new name: Openhold.

## History

Raised by hands long forgotten, the keep stood in ruin for years, home to nothing but the bandits who'd made it theirs. In the days after Marren Wick broke Lilith Woodborne's guard at Plains and took the Circlet of Command and the Darkest Secret, a stonemason travelling with her recognized what the ruin could still be. Marren drove the bandits out and set the mason to rebuilding it properly — walls first, then a gate — meant to shelter her own people rather than settle any old account.
