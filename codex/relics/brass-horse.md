# Brass Horse

## Description

A small cast-brass horse, heavy for its size and cold to the touch even in a warm hand. Reveal the top card of your discard pile and travel to a site with a card of that suit — or travel as normal if none is at hand — spending no Supply either way. 2 defence dice.

## History

Recovered from Shrouded Wood in Round 1 by Fia of the Ampa, burning one secret to bring it out from wherever it had sat hidden at the site — the first relic to leave Shrouded Wood since Fia took the site.

## Location

Carried by Fia of the Ampa (Purple), as of Round 1
