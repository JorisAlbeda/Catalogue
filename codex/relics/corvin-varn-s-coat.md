# Corvin Varn's Coat

## Description

A heavy travelling coat, cut too wide through the shoulders for whoever's currently wearing it — it was made for Corvin Varn's own frame, and nobody since has filled it out quite the same way. Patched twice at the elbow, worn soft from generations of daily use rather than display. Two things have been sewn permanently into its lining so far: a scrap of grey-green cloth — Isolde's Cloth (see that entry), taken up by Corvin once the Varn signet ring was sold off, in place of the ring rather than alongside it — and a page torn from a book of old tavern songs, added during Iona Ashe's own lifetime, its ink gone soft the way ink goes against wool worn daily. A second folded page, in a different hand than either, has been sent to hang alongside them as of Wynn Ashe's own time — see History.

The coat carries no habit of its own beyond the one stitched into it early and never unpicked since: never call a debt settled just because it's gone quiet. Whoever wears it, or simply keeps it hanging nearby, tends to pick the habit up along with the weight.

## History

Originally Corvin Varn's own coat, worn during the years he carried Isolde's Cloth sewn into its lining rather than loose in a pocket — marking in fabric what the sold-off Varn signet ring no longer could. Passed to the Ashe family through Corvin's friendship with Perrin Ashe, first Warden of Ashvale, rather than through blood; the family's own account of exactly when and why has softened some with retelling.

Generations later it reached Iona Ashe, who wore it through the rebellion that ended without her — too wide through the shoulders even on her, by her own family's account. During her own final season she traded a secret to the ruined magic at the Great Spire rather than take the relic it first offered her, and sewed the tavern-song page that trade bought into the coat's lining alongside Isolde's Cloth. She died before handing the coat to anyone directly. It hung at the Ashvale tavern hearth after, and the "never call a debt settled" habit passed down with it through her family in her place, several generations, until it reached Wynn Ashe.

Wynn never actually took this coat with him to Buried Giant — he wore his own plain travelling coat there instead, and left this one hanging at the tavern the whole time he was gone, same as it always had. Decades later, old and still living at Buried Giant, he had a copy made of the single folded page he'd personally carried since the war and sent it back to the tavern to hang alongside the coat, rather than travel there to add it himself — the words on it kept folded rather than repeated here.

## Location

Ashvale — hangs at the tavern hearth Wynn Ashe's family has kept for generations. Hasn't left that hearth since before Iona Ashe's own death.
