# The Circlet of Command

## Description

A plain ring, more iron than ornament, pried loose from the rubble at the Great Spire. Whoever wears it stands beyond the reach of anyone else's hands — no rival can take what it guards, banner or relic alike, so long as it stays on their finger.

## History

Buried at the Great Spire alongside the memorial to those lost at the Firestorm, the ring lay forgotten in the rubble until Lilith Woodborne pried it free there, paying for it with something she chose to let go of rather than keep. She wore it afterward as Chancellor, and it kept every other claim she carried — the Darkest Secret among them — out of reach of anyone who might have taken it from her.

It didn't keep the ring itself safe. At Plains, Marren Wick broke the last of Lilith's guards there and took it from her outright. With the ring off the Chancellor's hand, everything it had been shielding stopped being untouchable — Marren went straight for the Darkest Secret next.
