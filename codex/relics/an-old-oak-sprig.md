# A Sprig of the Old Oak

## Description

A hand's-length cutting, three leaves still green on it though it rides loose in a belt pouch rather than water or soil. It bends on its own, slow, the way a compass needle settles rather than snaps — toward Heart of the Oak most days, though it's been seen to lean toward the Emissary herself when she's close enough. Nobody's explained how a cutting keeps doing that without roots.

## History

Came to Senna Coldwell, Openhold's Truthwatcher, at a field edge rather than inside the Woodland Empire's own ground — she's never said more than that about where. She noticed early it turned toward Heart of the Oak the same mornings the border moved, and kept it as a facedown adviser rather than declare it, testing what it actually was before trusting it to anyone. When Fia of the Ampa visited Openhold in Year 540, she recognized it on sight and named it outright — "The Old Oak" — and told Senna to plant it herself, in ground of her own choosing, promising she'd know the place when she found it. Senna hasn't done so yet.

## Location

Openhold, carried by Senna Coldwell.
