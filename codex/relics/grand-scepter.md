# The Grand Scepter

## Description

The plain rod of office that's sat on every Truthwatcher's own desk since Marren Wick first claimed one, unremarkable enough that most of Openhold walks past it without a second look. It isn't kept locked away or displayed — it's a working tool, the same way Perrine's own ledger and measuring cord are, and she treats it exactly that way: something she uses, not something she guards.

## History

Perrine has carried it since the day she took the desk, the same as every Truthwatcher before her, and never once had occasion to use it for the harder half of what it's for — until this season, with Wynn Ashe holding the Darkest Secret and her own throne's footing under it plainly explained to her for the first time. She named it to him outright, at the edge of the Openhold Plains, over Aldous Ferry's company and everything else he'd spent without asking — told him she had it in hand, today, and every reason in front of her to use it before she got back on her horse. She didn't. Not because he talked her out of it and not because she'd forgiven anything — she said plainly she didn't trust herself to decide something that size in the same hour she was still angry enough to want to. She rode away with the question genuinely open, not settled, and the Scepter still sitting unused in her own hand.
