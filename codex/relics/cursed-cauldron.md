# Cursed Cauldron

## Description

A squat iron pot, rusted through in patches, its handle worked into a shape that might once have been a face before the rust took the detail. Sits wrong in the hand — too light where the iron looks thickest, too cold on a warm day. Whatever it's used to hold, it holds unwillingly.

## History

Said to have belonged to Corvin Varn in the years before Ashvale stood, back when he was still an exile carrying his grandmother's own unfinished death around with him like a debt. He kept it a single season, by the account that survives, before he had it carried as far from his own table as a rider could take it in a week — he never explained why beyond calling it a thing that gave him nightmares, and nobody who knew him well enough to ask ever did.

It surfaced generations later at Widowmire, half-sunk in the same mud the local mushrooms have never fully explained either, where Marek Sten dug it free the same day he marched on the bandit hold squatting there. Whatever ran off the two warbands he lost taking that ground wasn't the bandits themselves — soldiers who were there afterward described something that moved like a woman and screamed like a kettle left too long on the fire, and none of them have stopped calling the ground haunted since.

## Location

Widowmire, Provinces
